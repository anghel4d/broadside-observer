# Lineage: foundations-of-computing

Charter: the **epistemology of computation** — from Hilbert’s Entscheidungsproblem through Gödel incompleteness, Church–Turing effective calculability, von Neumann stored-program engineering, Shannon/Wiener information & cybernetics, Kleene/Post recursion theory, McCulloch–Pitts/Newell–Simon/McCarthy symbolic & neural models, then into programming-language semantics (Landin, Scott–Strachey, Hoare, Dijkstra) and algorithmic information (Kolmogorov/Chaitin). This is Anoptic/Broadside’s deep past: what it means for a process to be effective, a machine universal, a proof limited, a channel noisy, a language meaningful.

## Ordered spine

1. **Problem setting** — Hilbert’s problems / Hilbert–Ackermann Entscheidungsproblem; Frege–Peano–Principia formalization; Zermelo set theory.
2. **Incompleteness & proof theory** — Gödel completeness (1930) then incompleteness (1931); Gentzen sequent calculus; Dialectica; Tarski truth.
3. **Effective calculability (1936 cluster)** — Church λ / Entscheidungsproblem; Turing machines; Kleene general recursive functions; Post combinatory processes; equivalences & thesis.
4. **Combinators & types** — Schönfinkel; Curry; Church simple type theory & λ-conversion monograph.
5. **Stored-program engineering** — EDVAC draft; IAS Burks–Goldstine–von Neumann; ACE; EDSAC libraries; Hopper automatic programming; Zuse Plankalkül.
6. **Information, codes, cybernetics** — Shannon switching & MTC; Hamming/Golay/Huffman; Wiener cybernetics & feedback; McCulloch–Pitts.
7. **Automata & languages** — Kleene regular events; Rabin–Scott NFA; Chomsky hierarchy; Büchi; Moore/Mealy.
8. **Symbolic AI** — Logic Theorist / GPS / Physical Symbol System; McCarthy LISP & Advice Taker; Turing Mind 1950 / Intelligent Machinery.
9. **Programming as science** — Fortran / Algol 60 / BNF; Dijkstra structured programming & THE; Floyd–Hoare logic; Böhm–Jacopini; Knuth TAOCP.
10. **Mathematical semantics** — Landin SECD/ISWIM; Strachey fundamental concepts; Scott domains & Scott–Strachey denotational semantics; Plotkin/Hindley/Milner/Reynolds.
11. **Algorithmic information** — Kolmogorov / Chaitin / Solomonoff / Martin-Löf randomness.
12. **Self-reproduction & reliability** — von Neumann automata, SRA, probabilistic logics.
