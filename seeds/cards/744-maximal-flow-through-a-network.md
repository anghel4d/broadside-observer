# Maximal Flow Through a Network

## One-sentence takeaway

Ford–Fulkerson max-flow / min-cut via augmenting paths.

## Why it matters here

Flow networks model bandwidth, matching, and cut-based segmentation.

## Key ideas

- Residual graphs and augmenting paths.
- Max-flow equals min-cut.
- Integral flows with integral capacities.
- Launch point for Edmonds–Karp and Dinic.

## Caveats

- Pathological augmentations need shortest/capacity scaling.
- Real-valued capacities need care.
