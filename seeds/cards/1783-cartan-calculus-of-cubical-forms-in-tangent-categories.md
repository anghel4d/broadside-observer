# Cartan calculus of cubical forms in tangent categories

## One-sentence takeaway

Cartan calculus (exterior derivative, Lie derivative, interior product) for differential cubical forms, developed internally in a cartesian tangent category with scalars.

## Why it's lovely

Why you might love this: tangent categories already package "derivative" as structure; here the full Cartan calculus of forms arrives in cubical (not just simplicial/Koszul) clothing. Direct bridge from synthetic differential geometry to Discrete Exterior Calculus / FEEC instincts — and to Anoptic's geometry stack — without leaving the categorical language of 1582.

## Key ideas

- Cubical n-form: morphism TⁿX → R, antisymmetric and R-linear in each tangent factor.
- Differential forms: additionally equal to their own fibrewise derivative.
- Special case of Cruttwell–Lucyshyn-Wright singular forms.
- Full Cartan calculus developed in a cartesian tangent category with ring object R.
- Sits beside tangent-category Lie theory (1582), DEC (1028), and reverse-derivative categories (1056).

## Caveats

Synthetic/categorical; extracting a concrete mesh DEC algorithm still needs a discrete model. Cubical (vs exterior-algebra) presentation may feel unfamiliar at first.
