# The homotopy types of directed path and trace spaces

## One-sentence takeaway

A saturated directed space can have trace space homeomorphic to a square while the directed path space has nontrivial π₁ — so the canonical quotient is not a weak homotopy equivalence.

## Why it's lovely

Why you might love this: directed homotopy quietly assumes path spaces and trace spaces are 'the same up to homotopy.' Here they are not: traces look like a square, directed paths carry a loop. A sharp counterexample that anyone writing concurrent or directed type theory should keep in a pocket.

## Key ideas

- Constructs a saturated d-space with Hausdorff Δ-generated carrier.
- Trace space between two points ≅ square; directed path space has nontrivial fundamental group.
- Canonical quotient path→trace fails to be a weak homotopy equivalence.
- Separates two models often treated interchangeably in directed algebraic topology.

## Caveats

Existence/counterexample paper; does not give a classification of when path≃trace. Pair with directed-univalence card 1452 for Broadside's directed shelf, not as a citation of the same result.
