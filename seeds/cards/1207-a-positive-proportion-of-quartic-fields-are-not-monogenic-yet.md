# A positive proportion of quartic fields are not monogenic yet have no local obstruction to being so

## One-sentence takeaway

Positive proportion of quartic fields (and of quartic rings of integers) fail to be monogenic / fail to be invariant orders of binary quartics, with no local obstruction — the quartic upgrade of the cubic paper.

## Why it's lovely

Monogenic means O_K = Z[alpha]. Local-global failure for a classical invariant.

## Problem

After the cubic case, do quartics also fail monogeneity globally while locally fine?

## Design

Build on the cubic comparison of descents; Bhargava parametrizations of quartic rings / binary quartics.

## Evidence

## Limitations
