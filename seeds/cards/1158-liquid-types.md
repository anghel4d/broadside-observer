# Liquid Types

## One-sentence takeaway

Liquid Types combine Hindley–Milner with predicate abstraction for automatic refinement inference.

## Why it matters here

Root of LiquidHaskell—practical SMT-backed refinement typing for real programs.

## Key ideas

- HM plus predicate abstraction.
- SMT-backed subtyping.
- Minimal annotations for rich invariants.

## Caveats

- Part of the gradual/refinement type-safety shelf.
- Check mechanization status in follow-ons.
