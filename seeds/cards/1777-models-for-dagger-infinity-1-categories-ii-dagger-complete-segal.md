# Models for dagger $(\infty,1)$-categories II: Dagger complete Segal spaces and the Joyal--Tierney equivalence

## One-sentence takeaway

Part II completes a Quillen-equivalence chain among dagger Bergner, dagger Joyal, and dagger Rezk models for dagger (∞,1)-categories.

## Why it's lovely

Why you might love this: dagger categories are the home of unitary / adjoint-aware homotopy — the place where "inverse" is a structure, not a property. Getting complete Segal spaces and a Joyal–Tierney equivalence to play nicely with the dagger is spectator-sport ∞-category theory with immediate payoffs for reversible and quantum-flavoured higher algebra.

## Key ideas

- Builds on Part I's dagger Bergner and dagger Joyal model structures.
- Homotopically constant resolution model plus unitarily complete dagger Segal spaces.
- Change-of-index adjunction comparing presentations of dagger simplicial spaces.
- Dagger analogue of the Joyal–Tierney Quillen equivalence.
- Together with Part I: Quillen equivalences linking Bergner / Joyal / Rezk dagger models.

## Caveats

Model-category foundations; does not yet compute a specific dagger (∞,1)-category of interest. Read beside the Segal-space vs quasicategory shelf (1239) and synthetic ∞-category cards.
