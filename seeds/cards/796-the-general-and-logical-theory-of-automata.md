# The General and Logical Theory of Automata

## One-sentence takeaway

Von Neumann theorizes automata complexity, reliability, and self-reproduction precursors—computation as organism engineering.

## Why it matters here

Bridge from EDVAC organs to self-reproducing automata and reliable computation from unreliable parts.

## Key ideas

- Complexity thresholds for automata.
- Comparison with natural organisms.
- Sets up later probabilistic logics & self-reproduction.
- Hixon Symposium lecture.

## Caveats

- Classic; read primary text before overclaiming modern interpretations.
