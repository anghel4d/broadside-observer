# Koszul duality and Morita categories

## One-sentence takeaway

(∞,2)-Koszul duality for operads/cooperads in spectra as an equivalence of Morita categories of (co)operads and bi(co)modules.

## Why it's lovely

Why you might love this: Koszul duality upgraded to an (∞,2)-Morita story — operads and cooperads dualize by swapping bimodules for bicomodules. Same shelf energy as Dwyer–Hess / Day operads (1588), but now the duality itself is the Morita equivalence.

## Key ideas

- Morita category of operads and bimodules ↔ dual Morita category of cooperads and bicomodules.
- Works for operads/cooperads in spectra at (∞,2)-level.
- Subsumes several classical Koszul-duality forms as instances.
- Clean packaging: duality as equivalence of module categories, not just a chain-level formula.

## Caveats

Spectra / higher-categorical technical overhead; not a hands-on rewrite of classical GK Koszul duality for beginners.

## Implications for Broadside

Strengthens the operad / 2-monoidal shelf already seeded (1588). Useful mental model whenever Broadside talks about dualizing compositional structure.
