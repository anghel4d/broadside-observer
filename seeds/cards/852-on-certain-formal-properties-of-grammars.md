# On Certain Formal Properties of Grammars

## One-sentence takeaway

Chomsky relates grammar types to generative power—the Chomsky hierarchy.

## Why it matters here

Type-0..3 classification still organizes PL and parser theory.

## Key ideas

- Type 0–3 grammars.
- Containment of language classes.
- Ties to automata models.

## Caveats

- Classic; read primary text before overclaiming modern interpretations.
