# Crossed-module crossed braided categories

## One-sentence takeaway

For a crossed module χ: G→H, χ-crossed braided (and ribbon) categories unify G-crossed braided, G-graded braided, and G-acted braided categories in one package.

## Why it's lovely

Why you might love this: crossed modules are already the algebraic avatar of 2-groups; giving them a braided/ribbon categorical home is the natural next move after G-crossed braided categories. One definition that specialises to three classical notions — and sits next to the fusion-2-category / TQFT shelf (1646, 1617).

## Key ideas

- χ-crossed braided/ribbon categories: G-graded with H-action for χ: G→H.
- χ = id recovers classical G-crossed braided categories.
- χ: G→{∗} (abelian G) recovers G-graded braided categories.
- χ: {∗}→G recovers braided categories with a G-action.
- Equivalence relation is typically finer than for ordinary G-crossed braided categories.

## Caveats

Definitional/foundational; concrete TQFT constructions from χ-crossed ribbon categories are future work. Pair with graded fusion 2-categories (1646).
