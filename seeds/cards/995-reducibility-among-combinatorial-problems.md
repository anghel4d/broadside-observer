# Reducibility Among Combinatorial Problems

## One-sentence takeaway

Twenty-one combinatorial problems shown NP-complete via Karp reductions from SAT.

## Why it matters here

The catalogue that made NP-completeness a daily engineering concern.

## Key ideas

- Many-one polynomial reductions.
- Clique, Hamiltonian cycle, chromatic number, knapsack, etc.
- Template for proving new problems hard.
- Complements Cook’s SAT completeness.

## Caveats

- Historical list — Garey&Johnson expanded massively.
- Approximation-preserving reductions are a later concern.
