# On the Expressive Power of Programming Languages

## One-sentence takeaway

Formalizes when a language feature truly increases expressive power via macro-expressibility.

## Why it matters here

The expressiveness framework for arguing whether a control/effect feature is sugar or a real extension.

## Key ideas

- Macro-expressibility as the criterion.
- Continuations and control operators as case studies.
- Separates syntactic sugar from semantic extension.
- Companion to Felleisen calculi and CEK machine work.
