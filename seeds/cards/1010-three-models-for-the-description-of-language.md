# Three Models for the Description of Language

## One-sentence takeaway

Chomsky’s hierarchy framing — finite-state, phrase-structure, and transformational models.

## Why it matters here

Organizes the automata/grammar ladder compilers and DSL designers still use.

## Key ideas

- Finite-state vs phrase-structure capacity.
- Early generative-grammar computational view.
- Seeds regular/CF/CS/unrestricted hierarchy.
- Cross-field linguistics↔CS impact.

## Caveats

- Linguistic claims evolved; CS uses the hierarchy narrowly.
- Not an algorithms paper per se.
