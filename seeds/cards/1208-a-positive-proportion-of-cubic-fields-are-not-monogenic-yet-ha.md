# A positive proportion of cubic fields are not monogenic yet have no local obstruction to being so

## One-sentence takeaway

Compare 2-descent and 3-descent on Mordell curves y^2=x^3+k: a positive proportion of cubic fields are non-monogenic with no local obstruction, and Sha[3] can be arbitrarily large on a positive-density set.

## Why it's lovely

Monogeneity as a Selmer comparison. By-product: for every r>=0, a positive proportion of E_k have dim_{F_3} Sha[3] >= r.

## Problem

Which cubic fields are monogenic? Local conditions are insufficient.

## Design

2- vs 3-descent on y^2=x^3+k, ordered by height.

## Evidence

arXiv:2011.01186. Math. Ann. 2025, DOI 10.1007/s00208-024-03054-w.

## Limitations

Cubic only; quartic is 1207.
