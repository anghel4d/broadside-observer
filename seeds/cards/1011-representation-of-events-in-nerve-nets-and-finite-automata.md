# Representation of Events in Nerve Nets and Finite Automata

## One-sentence takeaway

Regular events and Kleene algebra — regular expressions equivalent to finite automata.

## Why it matters here

Theoretical root of every regex engine and lexer generator.

## Key ideas

- Closure under union, concat, star.
- Equivalence with finite automata.
- Historical nerve-net motivation.
- Kleene star as distinctive operator.

## Caveats

- Nerve-net framing is not modern ML.
- Practical regex backrefs exceed regular languages.
