# Fundamentals of binaural technology

## One-sentence takeaway

Møller's 1992 survey of binaural technology: recording, HRTF, headphone reproduction, and the equalization chain you still need.

## Why it matters here

Before MagLS and SOFA, this is the engineering checklist: blocked-meatus vs open-ear, headphone compensation, and why a raw KEMAR IR is not 'ready to convolve.' Anoptic headphone path should treat EQ as first-class, not an afterthought.

## Key ideas

- **Binaural chain.** Capture → HRTF/BRIR → headphone transfer function compensation.
- **Measurement practice.** What a dummy-head recording actually is.
- **Applied Acoustics 1992.** ~312 cites; pedagogy more than a new algorithm.
- **Still the vocabulary** for later measurement reviews (Li & Peissig 2020, 1689).

## Caveats

Pre-CIPIC/SADIE/SOFA. Analog-era measurement. Use as orientation, not as an HRTF dataset. Not a renderer.
