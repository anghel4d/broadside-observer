# Eff: Algebraic Effect Handlers Language

## One-sentence takeaway

Eff was an early executable language showcasing Plotkin/Pretnar handlers outside pure theory.

## Why it matters here

Craft companion to the handlers papers and Koka.

## Key ideas

- Handlers as user-programmable control.
- Direct implementation of algebraic effects ideas.
- Pedagogical bridge into later industrial effect systems.
- Same Pretnar lineage as Handling Algebraic Effects.

## Caveats

- Research language; ecosystem small vs OCaml 5 effects.
- Pin docs version when comparing semantics.
