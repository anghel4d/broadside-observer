# Gold-medalist Performance in Solving Olympiad Geometry with AlphaGeometry2

## One-sentence takeaway

AG2: Gemini LM plus expanded domain language plus SKEST search-tree sharing — 84% of IMO 2000-2024 geometry (was 54%), past average gold, and the geometry half of IMO 2024 silver.

## Why it matters here

The named upgrade. Coverage 66% to 88%; IMO-AG-50 solve 42/50.

## Problem

AG1 language missed motions, linear angle/ratio/distance equations, non-constructive problems.

## Design

Gemini architecture, faster DDAR, SKEST (shared knowledge ensemble of search trees), more synthetic data. Progress toward natural-language input.

## Evidence

arXiv:2502.03544, Feb 2025. Code: google-deepmind/alphageometry2. IMO 2024 P4.

## Limitations

Still geometry. Combinatorics half of IMO 2024 stayed open (see AlphaProof).

## Implications for Broadside

See 1221 and 1223. One card for AG2, not a blog duplicate.

## Bottom line

Mint 2502.03544.
