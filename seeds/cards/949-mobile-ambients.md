# Mobile Ambients

## One-sentence takeaway

Introduces mobile ambients: nested administrative domains that move, controlling locality in mobile computation.

## Why it matters here

Ambient calculus classic — locality/mobility card beyond bare π-calculus name-passing.

## Key ideas

- Ambients as bounded places.
- In/out/open capabilities for movement and dissolution.
- Model for firewalls, agents, and administrative domains.
- Companion to π-calculus and join-calculus.
