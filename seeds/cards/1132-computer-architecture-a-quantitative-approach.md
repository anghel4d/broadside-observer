# Computer Architecture: A Quantitative Approach

## One-sentence takeaway

H&P make quantitative, metric-driven architecture the standard: Amdahl, locality, and real workloads over anecdote.

## Why it matters here

The architecture textbook card—how to reason about CPU/GPU/memory tradeoffs for engines.

## Key ideas

- Quantitative design principles and metrics.
- Instruction sets, pipelining, memory hierarchy as measured design.
- Amdahl's law as a working tool.
- Multi-edition canon spanning RISC to multicore.

## Caveats

- Textbook card—pin edition when citing figures.
