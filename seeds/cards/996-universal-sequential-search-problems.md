# Universal Sequential Search Problems

## One-sentence takeaway

Independent discovery of NP-completeness plus universal search for inversion problems.

## Why it matters here

Completes the Cook–Levin story and seeds Levin-style universal search.

## Key ideas

- NP-completeness arrived independently in the USSR.
- Universal search dovetails enumerations by runtime.
- Inversion/search framing.
- Often cited jointly as Cook–Levin.

## Caveats

- Original Russian venue; English expositions vary.
- Universal search is rarely the practical solver.
