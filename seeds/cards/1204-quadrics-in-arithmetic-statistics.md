# Quadrics in arithmetic statistics

## One-sentence takeaway

Reintroduce the circle method into Bhargava-style orbit counting so you can count on a quadric cut out by a quadratic invariant; worked example: average 2-Selmer is 3 on y^2=x^3+B and y^2=x^3+B^2.

## Why it's lovely

The method paper behind the two-cubes work. Smoothed Bhargava counting plus a deduction that the constrained average is 3 from the unconstrained one.

## Problem

Arithmetic statistics on invariant quadrics — the locus the usual geometry-of-numbers wants to avoid.

## Design

Circle method times Bhargava. Formal deduction of the average 3 from unconstrained families.

## Evidence

arXiv:2110.03947, v2 14 Oct 2024.
