# The Chemical Abstract Machine

## One-sentence takeaway

Introduces the Chemical Abstract Machine (CHAM) metaphor for concurrent rewriting and process interaction.

## Why it matters here

CHAM is the memorable concurrent-semantics metaphor that fed join-calculus and teaching process calculi.

## Key ideas

- Solutions, molecules, and reactions as concurrency imagery.
- Heating/cooling rules for structural congruence.
- Flexible framework for defining process calculi.
- Influences join calculus and chemical programming narratives.
