# The Morita classes are nonzero

## One-sentence takeaway

All Morita classes μ_k in H_{4k}(Aut(F_{2k+2}); ℚ) are nonzero — disproving a case of Kontsevich's finiteness conjecture via Steinberg homology's Hopf structure.

## Why it's lovely

Why you might love this: Kontsevich asked whether these Morita classes might vanish for large k; they do not. A novel Hopf-algebra description of Steinberg homology turns a finiteness rumour into an infinite family of nonzero stable-homology classes — spectator-sport algebra with a conjecture corpse on the table.

## Key ideas

- Morita classes μ_k ∈ H_{4k}(Aut(F_{2k+2}); ℚ) are shown nonzero for all k.
- Proof uses a new description of the Hopf algebra structure on Steinberg homology.
- Analogous nonvanishing with twisted coefficients.
- Disproves a case of Kontsevich's finiteness conjecture on these classes.

## Caveats

Homology of automorphism groups of free groups; does not by itself compute mapping-class or Out(F_n) phenomena Broadside builds with. Pair with Morita-category card 1616 for naming, not for the same theorem.
