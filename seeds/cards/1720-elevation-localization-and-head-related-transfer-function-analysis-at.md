# Elevation localization and head-related transfer function analysis at low frequencies

## One-sentence takeaway

Low-frequency elevation cues are mostly torso/head-shadow, not pinna notches — Algazi/Avendano/Duda 2001 JASA.

## Why it matters here

Cheap LF HRTF personalization can use head/torso size (CIPIC anthropometry) without a pinna scan. This paper justifies snowman/ellipsoid models (1721) at low frequency and why generic KEMAR bass isn't crazy.

## Key ideas

- **Elevation at LF.** Torso reflection / head diffraction dominate.
- **JASA 2001.** Same CIPIC authors, companion to the database paper.
- **Personalization hint.** Anthropometry → LF HRTF.
- **~153 cites.** Harvest personalization/anthropometry.

## Caveats

High-frequency elevation is still pinna and *does* need individual or SADIE-class data. Not a full HRTF model. Not near-field.
