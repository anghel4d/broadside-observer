# Graded-fusion 2-categories and quantum homotopy invariants of 4-manifolds

## One-sentence takeaway

3-group-graded extensions of fusion 2-categories yield state-sum invariants of 4-manifolds with maps to a homotopy 3-type.

## Why it's lovely

Why you might love this: fusion categories already power 3D TQFTs; here the 2-categorical lift gets graded by a 3-group (modeled as a 2-crossed module) and pays out quantum-homotopy invariants of 4-manifolds. Pure "higher categories as physics machinery" delight, complementary to the derived-MTC 3D TQFT card (1617).

## Key ideas

- 3-group-graded extensions of fusion 2-categories via 2-crossed modules.
- State-sum invariants of 4-manifolds equipped with a map to a homotopy 3-type.
- Equivalently: invariants of flat 3-bundles over 4-manifolds.
- Lifts the Reshetikhin–Turaev / Turaev–Viro story one dimension.

## Caveats

Heavy higher-categorical machinery; computational examples may be sparse. Pair with 1617 (3D TQFTs from derived quantum-group categories) rather than treating as a drop-in engine technique.
