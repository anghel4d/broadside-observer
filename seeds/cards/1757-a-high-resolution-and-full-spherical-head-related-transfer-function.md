# A High Resolution and Full-Spherical Head-Related Transfer Function Database for Different Head-Above-Torso Orientations

## One-sentence takeaway

FABIAN: full-sphere HRTF database across *head-above-torso* orientations — Brinkmann/Lindau/Weinzierl et al. JAES 2017.

## Why it matters here

Players look around; the torso doesn't always follow. FABIAN is the measured set for that extra degree of freedom (TU Berlin dummy). Anoptic seated VR vs full-body: this vs CIPIC. Harvest measurement.

## Key ideas

- **Head-above-torso orientations.** Not only yaw of a rigid dummy.
- **Full-spherical grid.** High resolution.
- **JAES 2017.** DOI 10.17743/jaes.2017.0033.
- **Measured dummy (FABIAN).** Complements KEMAR/SADIE/HUTUBS.

## Caveats

Dummy, not individual subjects (that's HUTUBS/SONICOM). Far-field. Headphone IR still needed. Not a renderer.
