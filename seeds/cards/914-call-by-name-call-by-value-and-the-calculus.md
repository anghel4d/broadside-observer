# Call-by-Name, Call-by-Value and the λ-Calculus

## One-sentence takeaway

Separates call-by-name and call-by-value lambda calculi with matching operational and denotational accounts.

## Why it matters here

The evaluation-order classic — required before CBPV, monadic effects, or deciding what λ means in ano.

## Key ideas

- Distinct calculi for CBN vs CBV.
- Correspondence between reduction and semantic equality.
- Sets up later PCF and operational-semantics work.
- Constantly cited by Moggi/Wadler/Levy lines already in corpus.
