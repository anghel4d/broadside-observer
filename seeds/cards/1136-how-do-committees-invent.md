# How Do Committees Invent?

## One-sentence takeaway

Conway's law: system structure mirrors the communication structure of the organization that designed it.

## Why it matters here

Org/design isomorphism warning when splitting Broadside services and engine modules.

## Key ideas

- Organizations produce designs copying their communication graph.
- Committee invention dynamics.
- Implication: change the org to change the architecture.
- Still cited in microservice and modularity debates.

## Caveats

- Classic; verify claims against the primary text before engineering decisions.
