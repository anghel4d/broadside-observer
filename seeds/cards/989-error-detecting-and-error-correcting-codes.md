# Error Detecting and Error Correcting Codes

## One-sentence takeaway

Hamming distance and the (7,4) code made single-error correction constructive.

## Why it matters here

Canonical ECC primitive still reused in storage and network reliability stacks.

## Key ideas

- Hamming distance as detect/correct metric.
- Parity-check construction for SEC.
- Sphere-packing intuition.
- Ancestor of algebraic coding.

## Caveats

- Not capacity-approaching modern codes.
- Does not address burst or soft-decision decoding.
