## Key ideas

- *The Deligne-Drinfeld conjecture*: We prove the Deligne–Drinfeld conjecture: the rational Grothendieck–Teichmüller Lie algebra is freely generated by one element in every odd weight at least three, with the corresponding isomorphism after weight completion.
- Lean scope (lean/docs/008.md): The Deligne–Drinfeld conjecture predicts that the rational Grothendieck–Teichmüller Lie algebra is freely generated by one element in each odd weight $3,5,7,\ldots$. The formalization establishes this for the rational solution space of antisymmetry, the three-term equation, and the four-strand pentagon with the Ihara bracket.
