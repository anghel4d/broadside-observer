# Die Vollständigkeit der Axiome des logischen Funktionenkalküls

## One-sentence takeaway

Gödel's completeness theorem: every valid first-order sentence has a finite proof in the Hilbert–Ackermann calculus, so FOL validity and provability coincide.

## Why it matters here

Separates "logic is complete" from "arithmetic is incomplete"; Broadside epistemology cards need both theorems, in this order, from the same author.

## Key ideas

- Thesis 1929 / paper 1930 in *Monatshefte* (DOI 10.1007/BF01692469), answering the completeness question left open by *Grundzüge*.
- If a sentence is true in every model, a formal derivation exists; equivalently, a consistent set of sentences has a model.
- Completeness is about the *logical* calculus, not about the arithmetical axioms he will show incomplete a year later.
- English working text is usually van Heijenoort's *From Frege to Gödel* translation.
