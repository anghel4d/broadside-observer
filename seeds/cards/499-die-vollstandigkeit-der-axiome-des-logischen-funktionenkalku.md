# Die Vollständigkeit der Axiome des logischen Funktionenkalküls

## One-sentence takeaway

Gödel's completeness theorem: first-order validity coincides with provability—logic is complete even though arithmetic is not.

## Why it matters here

Separates 'logic is complete' from 'arithmetic is incomplete'; essential precision for Broadside epistemology cards.

## Key ideas

- Every valid FOL sentence has a finite proof.
- Model-theoretic and proof-theoretic coincidence for FOL.
- Thesis 1929 / paper 1930.

## Caveats

- DOI sometimes shared in indexes with nearby Gödel papers; confirm Monatshefte 1930 completeness vs 1931 incompleteness.
