# Une extension de l'interprétation de Gödel à l'analyse, et son application à l'élimination des coupures dans l'analyse et la théorie des types

## One-sentence takeaway

Girard's System F: second-order lambda calculus arising from proof theory and cut-elimination for second-order arithmetic.

## Why it matters here

The proof-theoretic twin of Reynolds polymorphism — essential for connecting type theory to logic in Broadside's maths/PL shelf.

## Key ideas

- System F as functional interpretation extending Gödel-style ideas.
- Impredicative polymorphism with strong normalization.
- Bridge from second-order arithmetic to typed lambda calculi.
- Later popularized via Proofs and Types and the 1986 retrospective.
