# Computers and Intractability: A Guide to the Theory of NP-Completeness

## One-sentence takeaway

Encyclopedic field guide to NP-completeness proofs and the famous open-problem appendix.

## Why it matters here

Still the first book pulled when classifying a new combinatorial bottleneck.

## Key ideas

- Standard reduction patterns.
- Huge catalogue of NP-complete problems.
- Advice on coping with intractability.
- Open problems shaped decades of work.

## Caveats

- Pre-PCP/approximation era.
- One card for the whole book by design.
