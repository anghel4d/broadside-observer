# The Global Spherical Shell conjecture

## One-sentence takeaway

OpenAI's result family 060 (Algebraic and complex geometry) claims: Every connected minimal compact complex surface of class VII with $b_2\gt 0$ contains a global spherical shell, proving the positive-b2 Global Spherical Shell conjecture.

## Why it matters here

## Key ideas

- Family summary (repo): Such a shell is a holomorphically embedded neighborhood of the standard three-sphere in $\mathbb C^2\setminus\{0\}$ whose complement is connected.
- *Global Spherical Shells on Minimal Surfaces of Class VII*: We prove the Global Spherical Shell conjecture: every connected minimal compact complex surface of class VII with positive second Betti number contains a global spherical shell. This is a holomorphically embedded neighborhood of the standard three-sphere in $\mathbb C^2\setminus\{0\}$ whose complement is connected.
