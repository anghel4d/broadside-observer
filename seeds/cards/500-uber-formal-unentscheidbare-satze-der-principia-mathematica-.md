# Über formal unentscheidbare Sätze der Principia Mathematica und verwandter Systeme I

## One-sentence takeaway

Any consistent formal system strong enough for arithmetic harbors true but unprovable sentences—and cannot prove its own consistency.

## Why it matters here

The hard stop on Hilbert's program; every Anoptic discussion of total verification or self-certifying systems sits downstream.

## Key ideas

- Gödel numbering / arithmetization of syntax.
- First incompleteness theorem.
- Second incompleteness theorem (consistency).
- Explicitly targets Principia-like systems.
