# Refined Criteria for Gradual Typing

## One-sentence takeaway

States the gradual guarantee: dropping annotations must preserve behavior up to blame.

## Why it matters here

Yardstick separating sound gradual systems from TypeScript-style best-effort checking.

## Key ideas

- Gradual guarantee as semantic criterion.
- Interplay with blame theorems.
- Optional typing is not gradual typing.

## Caveats

- Part of the gradual/refinement type-safety shelf.
- Check mechanization status in follow-ons.
