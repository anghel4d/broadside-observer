# The Integrability of a knife-edge Billiard in a Disk

## One-sentence takeaway

A knife-edge (nonholonomic) billiard in a disk is integrable: rotational symmetry reduces the impact map to an explicit integrable return.

## Why it's lovely

Why you might love this: ordinary billiards bounce; knife-edge billiards skate — a nonholonomic constraint that usually destroys integrability. Inside a disk the rotational symmetry is enough to prove the impact map integrable anyway. Tiny mechanical story, clean dynamical punchline, sits next to Broadside's two-centre and tiling-billiard cards.

## Key ideas

- Nonholonomic knife-edge constraint inside a circular table.
- Impact-space coordinates adapted to rotational symmetry.
- Post-impact return map shown integrable.
- Adds a constrained sibling to symplectic/tiling billiard shelf (1583, 1620).

## Caveats

Disk geometry is special; does not claim integrability for general tables or rough boundaries. Classical mechanics / DS paper, not a graphics technique.
