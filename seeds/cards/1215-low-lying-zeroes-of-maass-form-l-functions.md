# Low-lying zeroes of Maass form L-functions

## One-sentence takeaway

One-level density of level-1 Maass forms, as eigenvalues go to infinity, agrees with orthogonal type for test functions supported inside (-2+2/(2M+1), 2-2/(2M+1)), via Kuznetsov weights vanishing to order M at 0.

## Why it's lovely

The Maass sibling of Iwaniec-Luo-Sarnak's holomorphic-newform densities. Support past (-1,1) distinguishes the three orthogonal groups.

## Problem

Katz-Sarnak for GL2 Maass forms at the central point.

## Design

Smoothed Kuznetsov, vanishing-to-order-M weights, one-level density.

## Evidence

IMRN 2015, no. 10, 2678-2701. DOI 10.1093/imrn/rnu012. arXiv:1301.5702.

## Limitations

Level 1; support depends on M. See 1216 for the family-with-level variant.

## Implications for Broadside

Miller-collaboration analytic-NT card.

## Bottom line

Mint IMRN.
