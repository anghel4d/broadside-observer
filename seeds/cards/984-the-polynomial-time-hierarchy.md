# The Polynomial-Time Hierarchy

## One-sentence takeaway

Defines the polynomial-time hierarchy as the scaled analogue of the arithmetic hierarchy.

## Why it matters here

Organizes oracles/quantifiers above NP used in hardness discussions.

## Key ideas

- Σ_k^p / Π_k^p via alternating quantifiers.
- Oracle characterization.
- Collapses and consequences.
- Standard map above NP/coNP.

## Caveats

- Whether PH is infinite is open.
- Not every practical problem needs PH machinery.
