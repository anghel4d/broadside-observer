# Advanced version of Gemini with Deep Think officially achieves gold-medal standard at the International Mathematical Olympiad

## One-sentence takeaway

Gemini Deep Think, end-to-end natural language, contest time: 35/42 on IMO 2025, official IMO graders, gold-medal standard — the certified twin of OpenAI's 35/42 announcement.

## Why it matters here

2025 gold, official. Unlike 2024 AlphaProof (Lean, days, expert formalization), this is informal proofs from the official statements inside 4.5 hours.

## Problem

Silver 2024 was formal and slow. Can a general model do IMO in natural language on the clock?

## Design

Deep Think: parallel exploration of solution candidates rather than a single chain. No tools. Same constraints as humans.

## Evidence

DeepMind blog, July 2025. IMO graders: "clear, precise and most of them easy to follow." Solutions released. Same 35/42 as OpenAI's experimental model (1229).

## Limitations

"Advanced version" not the public Gemini 2.5 Pro (which scored far lower without the pipeline). P6 unsolved. Blog, not a Nature paper (yet).

## Implications for Broadside

The certified 2025 gold. See 1223 for 2024 silver and 1229 for OpenAI's parallel claim.

Mint the DeepMind blog. Do not remint as AlphaProof.
