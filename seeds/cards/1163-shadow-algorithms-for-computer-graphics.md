# Shadow Algorithms for Computer Graphics

## One-sentence takeaway

Crow surveys and develops shadow algorithms including shadow volumes—silhouette-extruded stencil shadows.

## Why it matters here

Shadow-volume counterpart to Williams maps; still taught beside stencil shadows.

## Key ideas

- Shadow volume geometry from silhouettes.
- Taxonomy of early shadow approaches.
- Object-precision shadows vs image methods.
- Sets up Carmack/Heidmann stencil popularization later.

## Caveats

- Classic; verify claims against the primary text before engineering decisions.
