# On pushouts of strict symmetric monoidal categories

## One-sentence takeaway

An explicit pushout of strict symmetric monoidal categories (strong monoidal functors) plus the fact that braided strong monoidal functors preserve central idempotents — aimed at modelling concurrency.

## Why it's lovely

Why you might love this: central idempotents in monoidal categories are a crisp algebraic handle on "independent subsystems," and pushouts are how you glue them. Explicit constructions here are rare and useful; the concurrency modelling intent is a welcome bridge toward process/effect semantics without leaving pure CT.

## Key ideas

- Explicit pushout construction for strict SMCs along strong monoidal functors.
- Any braided strong monoidal functor strongly preserves central idempotents.
- Corollary: lattice of central idempotents is isomorphic (as meet-semilattice) to that of the strictification.
- Motivated by modelling computational concurrency via central idempotents.

## Caveats

Strict SMC setting; weak/monoidal bicategorical pushouts remain harder. Concurrency application is motivational rather than a finished process calculus.
