# Recursively enumerable sets of positive integers and their decision problems

## One-sentence takeaway

Post's problem about simple sets and degrees launches degree theory—structure inside the undecidable.

## Why it matters here

Shows undecidability is not binary; r.e. degrees become a landscape.

## Key ideas

- Creative / simple / hypersimple sets.
- Post's problem (is there an incomplete r.e. degree?).
- Decision problems for r.e. sets.
- Founding degree theory.

## Caveats

- Classic; read primary text before overclaiming modern interpretations.
