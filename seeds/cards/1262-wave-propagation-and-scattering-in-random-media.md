# Wave Propagation and Scattering in Random Media

## One-sentence takeaway

Ishimaru's Wave Propagation and Scattering in Random Media — the radiative-transfer / diffusion handbook SSS borrowed.

## Problem

Chandrasekhar is plane-parallel atmospheres. Tissue and paint need random-media scattering, transport approximation, and diffusion.

## Design

Two-volume treatise: RTE, transport approximation, diffusion, multiple scattering in random continua. Academic Press 1978; IEEE reprint DOI 10.1109/9780470547045.

## Evidence

1978, DOI 10.1109/9780470547045 (IEEE reprint). Lineage: Chandrasekhar → Ishimaru → Farrell / Stam / Jensen.

## Limitations

Physics handbook, not a renderer. Graphics cites the diffusion and RTE chapters.
