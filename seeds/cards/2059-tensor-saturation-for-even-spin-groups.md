- Family summary (repo): This resolves the type-D part of the simply-laced saturation conjecture.
- *Tensor saturation for even spin groups*: We prove the saturation conjecture for $\mathop{\mathrm{Spin}}\nolimits (2n)$, n ≥ 2.
