# The Geometry Engine: A VLSI Geometry System for Graphics

## One-sentence takeaway

Clark's Geometry Engine: VLSI pipeline for transforms/clipping—the hardware seed of SGI and the fixed-function GPU.

## Why it matters here

Explains why the graphics pipeline became hardware stages long before programmable shaders.

## Key ideas

- 12-chip VLSI geometry pipeline concept.
- Transform, clip, project in hardware.
- Commercial path via SGI.
- Ancestor of fixed-function T&L units.

## Caveats

- Classic; verify claims against the primary text before engineering decisions.
