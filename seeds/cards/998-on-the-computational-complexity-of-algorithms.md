# On the Computational Complexity of Algorithms

## One-sentence takeaway

Founded computational complexity with time-bounded Turing machines and hierarchy ideas.

## Why it matters here

Historical root of resource-bounded algorithm analysis beyond decidability.

## Key ideas

- Multi-tape TM time measures.
- Speed-up and hierarchy phenomena.
- Complexity classes as objects of study.
- Bridge from computability to complexity.

## Caveats

- Model details differ from modern RAM analyses.
- Hierarchy theorems sharpened later.
