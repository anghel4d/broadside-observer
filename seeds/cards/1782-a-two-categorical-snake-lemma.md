# A two-categorical Snake Lemma

## One-sentence takeaway

The Snake Lemma lifts to 2-categories: 2-kernels/cokernels, a 2-dimensional Normal Short Five Lemma, and a characterisation of homologically self-dual 2-categories.

## Why it's lovely

Why you might love this: the Snake Lemma is the emblematic diagram chase of homological algebra; watching it survive — carefully — in a 2-category with bizero objects is pure foundational delight. Dinversion, Pure Snake, and Third Isomorphism as equivalent characterisations of homological self-duality is a tidy package.

## Key ideas

- 2-kernels/cokernels; 2-monos/epis as fully faithful / cofully faithful 1-cells.
- Short 2-exact sequences and normal image factorisations.
- Two-dimensional Normal Short Five Lemma.
- Dinversion of an antinormal pair; homologically self-dual 2-categories.
- Equivalence of: homology self-duality ↔ Pure Snake Lemma ↔ Third Isomorphism Property.

## Caveats

2-categorical foundations; concrete examples (e.g. Cat, abelian categories as a 2-category) need care with size and enrichment. Companion to exactness-of-2-categories work in the same orbit.
