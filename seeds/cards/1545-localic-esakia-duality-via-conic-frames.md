# Localic Esakia Duality via Conic Frames

## One-sentence takeaway

Esakia duality goes fully constructive and localic: Heyting frames + conic frames recover the algebra without the Prime Ideal Theorem.

## Why it's lovely

Why you might love this: classical Esakia duals of Heyting algebras lean on the Prime Ideal Theorem — choice you cannot run in a constructive or type-theoretic setting. van der Schaaf rebuilds the dual on locales (Townsend-style Priestley) so the spectrum is a frame, not a space that needs ultrafilters. Logic that stays honest about what it assumes.

## Key ideas

- Classical Esakia: Heyting algebras ↔ Esakia spaces; recovery of the algebra uses PIT (non-constructive).
- Localic rewrite: Heyting frames on the algebraic side; conic frames as the dual geometric objects.
- Builds on Townsend's localic Priestley duality; the Esakia specialization is the new piece.
- Fully constructive: no ultrafilters, no Boolean Prime Ideal Theorem.

## Caveats

Localic duals are frames / locales, not spectral spaces with points; readers wanting classical point-set Esakia still need PIT. "Recently introduced Heyting frames" — check the cited source for the exact axiomatics.
