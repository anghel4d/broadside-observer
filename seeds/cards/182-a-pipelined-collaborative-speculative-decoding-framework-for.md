## Key ideas

- Recent advancements and widespread adoption of Large Language Models (LLMs) in both industry and academia have catalyzed significant demand for LLM serving.
- However, traditional cloud services incur high costs, while on-device inference alone faces challenges due to limited resources.
- Edge-cloud collaboration emerges as a key research direction to combine the strengths of both paradigms, yet efficiently utilizing limited network bandwidth while fully leveraging and balancing the computational capabilities of edge devices and the cloud remains an open problem.
- To address these challenges, we propose Pipelined Collaborative Speculative Decoding Framework (PicoSpec), a novel, general-purpose, and training-free speculative decoding framework for LLM edge-cloud collaborative inference.
