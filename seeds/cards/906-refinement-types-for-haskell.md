# Refinement Types for Haskell

## One-sentence takeaway

LiquidHaskell brings liquid refinement types to Haskell with SMT and measures.

## Why it matters here

Living refinement system for a production language—model for verifying without full Π-types.

## Key ideas

- Measures and refined constructors.
- Laziness subtleties.
- SMT automation over Haskell.

## Caveats

- Part of the gradual/refinement type-safety shelf.
