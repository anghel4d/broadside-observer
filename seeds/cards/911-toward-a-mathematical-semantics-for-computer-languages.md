# Toward a Mathematical Semantics for Computer Languages

## One-sentence takeaway

Scott–Strachey program: give programming languages a compositional mathematical semantics via domains.

## Why it matters here

Founding document of denotational semantics — pairs Strachey concepts with Scott domain theory.

## Key ideas

- Compositional semantic functions into domains.
- Continuations/stores as semantic ingredients.
- Program for a mathematical theory of computation for PLs.
- Leads to Scott's domain-theoretic solutions to recursive domain equations.
