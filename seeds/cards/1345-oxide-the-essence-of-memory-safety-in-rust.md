# Oxide: The Essence of Memory Safety in Rust

## One-sentence takeaway

Oxide is a formal mid-level Rust calculus capturing ownership and borrow checking.

## Why it matters here

Syntactic type-system model of Rust's essence—cleaner than full rustc for extensions.

## Key ideas

- Mid-level calculus.
- Borrow checking formalized.
- Memory-safety theorems.
