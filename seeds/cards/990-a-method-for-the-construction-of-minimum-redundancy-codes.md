# A Method for the Construction of Minimum-Redundancy Codes

## One-sentence takeaway

Optimal prefix codes via greedy merge of least-probable symbols.

## Why it matters here

Default entropy-coder building block before arithmetic/ANS in engines and tools.

## Key ideas

- Greedy tree yields minimum expected length among prefix codes.
- Kraft inequality feasibility.
- Ties to Shannon rates.
- Static and adaptive variants.

## Caveats

- Symbol-by-symbol; arithmetic/ANS can beat redundancy.
- Needs known or estimated probabilities.
