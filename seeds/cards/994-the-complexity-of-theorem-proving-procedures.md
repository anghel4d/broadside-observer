# The Complexity of Theorem-Proving Procedures

## One-sentence takeaway

SAT is NP-complete — the Cook–Levin theorem that founded NP-completeness.

## Why it matters here

Root hardness result behind almost every intractability argument Anoptic touches.

## Key ideas

- Polynomial-time reducibility as comparison tool.
- SAT captures nondeterministic polynomial time.
- Tableau encoding into CNF.
- Launches the NP-completeness program.

## Caveats

- Does not imply P≠NP.
- Fine structure of reductions came later.
