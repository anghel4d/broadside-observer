# Analytic rigidity and symbolic dynamics for two-centre billiards

## One-sentence takeaway

Sharp rigidity–chaos dichotomy for planar two-centre billiards: only confocal ellipses stay integrable at every energy — a Birkhoff–Poritsky analogue with two foci.

## Why it's lovely

Why you might love this: billiards again, but now two centres and a rigidity theorem with teeth. Continues the symplectic/tiling billiards delight from 1583 with a classical-mechanics punchline about when integrability survives.

## Key ideas

- Analogue of Birkhoff–Poritsky: integrable-at-every-energy tables should be ellipses confocal with the two centres.
- Analytic rigidity results for the integrable locus.
- Symbolic dynamics / chaos on the complementary side of the dichotomy.
- Planar two-centre billiards as the model system.

## Caveats

Analytic / smooth-category hypotheses on the table boundary; not a numerical recipe for chaotic billiard art.
