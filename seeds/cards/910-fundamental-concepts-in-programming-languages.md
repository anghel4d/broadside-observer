# Fundamental Concepts in Programming Languages

## One-sentence takeaway

Strachey's 1967 lectures naming L-values/R-values, environments, and the conceptual toolkit of denotational semantics.

## Why it matters here

The vocabulary paper for PL semantics — still how we talk about stores, environments, and abstract syntax.

## Key ideas

- L-values vs R-values; environments and stores.
- Abstract syntax as the object of semantics.
- Programmed in the CPL/Scott–Strachey tradition.
- Published widely via the 2000 HOSC reprint.
