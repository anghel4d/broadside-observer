# Decidability and shortest strings in formal languages

## One-sentence takeaway

Given a language specified in various ways, decide emptiness and bound the shortest word — high-school / early-college work with Shallit, before the number-theory turn.

## Why it's lovely

The formal-languages fossil in an otherwise arithmetic-geometry corpus. Real paper, LNCS, Shallit.

## Problem

Emptiness and shortest-word length for languages under several presentations.

## Design

Automata / descriptional-complexity arguments; upper and lower bounds.

## Evidence

DCFS 2011, LNCS 6808, 55-67. DOI 10.1007/978-3-642-22600-7_5. arXiv:1103.1622.

## Limitations

Not number theory. Included because it is a sourced research paper on the homepage.
