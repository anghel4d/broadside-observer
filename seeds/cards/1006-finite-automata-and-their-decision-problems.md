# Finite Automata and Their Decision Problems

## One-sentence takeaway

Nondeterministic finite automata and the subset construction — Rabin–Scott.

## Why it matters here

Foundation of lexical analysis, protocol regtests, and formal language tooling.

## Key ideas

- NFA as a first-class model.
- Subset construction to DFA.
- Decision problems for finite automata.
- Nondeterminism as a design tool.

## Caveats

- Exponential blow-up is tight in worst case.
- Does not cover pushdown power.
