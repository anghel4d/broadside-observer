# Twisted Bicategorical Shadows and Traces

## One-sentence takeaway

Bicategorical shadows — the Morita-invariant home of Hochschild and THH — get a twisted upgrade that still carries traces.

## Why it's lovely

Why you might love this: shadows are the clean categorical reason Hochschild homology (and THH) is Morita-invariant, and the place where "trace" graduates from symmetric monoidal categories to bicategories. Twisting them equivariantly without breaking that story is spectator-sport category theory with immediate THH payoffs.

## Key ideas

- Bicategorical shadows unify HH / THH and encode Morita invariance.
- Traces extend from symmetric monoidal categories to bicategories via shadows.
- Twisted / equivariant variants that still support the shadow axioms.
- Connects to the Balmer / Morita / THH shelf already in the library.

## Caveats

Foundational/categorical; does not compute new THH of a specific ring spectrum. Read beside Morita / Koszul duality cards (1616) and THH-adjacent K-theory work.
