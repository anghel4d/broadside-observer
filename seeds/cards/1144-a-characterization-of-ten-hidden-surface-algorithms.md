# A Characterization of Ten Hidden-Surface Algorithms

## One-sentence takeaway

Sutherland–Sproull–Schumacker taxonomize ten hidden-surface algorithms—sorting in object vs image space as the key dichotomy.

## Why it matters here

Visibility taxonomy that still frames why Z-buffer won and when analytic algorithms matter.

## Key ideas

- Object-space vs image-space sorting.
- Ten-algorithm characterization.
- Criteria: faces, edges, complexity.
- Sets stage for Z-buffer dominance.
