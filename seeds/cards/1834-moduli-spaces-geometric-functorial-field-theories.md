# Moduli spaces of geometric functorial field theories

## One-sentence takeaway

Moduli of geometric functorial field theories are computed as mapping spaces of O(d)-equivariant simplicial presheaves via Cartesian realization of geometric structures.

## Why it's lovely

Why you might love this: functorial field theories are TQFTs with geometry still attached — Riemannian, conformal, whatever the site encodes. This paper turns their moduli into actual mapping spaces you can compute with, via a Cartesian realization of a geometric structure presheaf. Bridge between Stolz–Teichner dreams and something you can write a spectral-sequence for.

## Key ideas

- Geometric structures as presheaves on smooth families of d-manifolds.
- Cartesian realization → O(d)-equivariant simplicial presheaf.
- Moduli of geometric FFTs ≃ mapping spaces in that equivariant setting.
- Toolkit aimed at computing rather than only defining geometric field theories.

## Caveats

Infrastructure paper; concrete computations of famous moduli (e.g. specific d and F) may be deferred. No safe preexisting Broadside stem for geometric FFT moduli — see left empty.
