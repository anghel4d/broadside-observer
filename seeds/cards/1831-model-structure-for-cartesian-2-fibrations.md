# A model structure for cartesian 2-fibrations

## One-sentence takeaway

Marked 2-categories carry a model structure whose fibrant objects present cartesian 2-fibrations, replacing much weak-coherence bookkeeping by a strict homotopical model.

## Why it's lovely

Why you might love this: indexed categories via cartesian 2-fibrations usually drown in coherence. Marking 2-categories and building a model structure lets straightening / comprehension live in a Quillen world — the same move that made quasicategories tractable, now one dimension up for people who index things for a living.

## Key ideas

- Cartesian 2-fibrations as a model of indexed categories.
- Marked 2-categories record the cartesian lifts strictly enough for a model structure.
- Homotopical replacement for classical straightening coherence data.
- Targets (∞,2)-adjacent bookkeeping without full ∞-machinery in the paper's surface.

## Caveats

Model-category foundations; comparison functors to fully weak (∞,2) presentations may still be needed for applications. No preexisting Broadside stem for 2-fibrations — see left empty rather than invented.
