# A Structural Approach to Operational Semantics

## One-sentence takeaway

Introduces Structural Operational Semantics (SOS): syntax-directed inference rules for program execution.

## Why it matters here

SOS is how modern language defs and type-safety proofs are written — the Plotkin AIS notes are the root.

## Key ideas

- Structural (syntax-directed) transition rules.
- Alternative to purely denotational definitions.
- Scales to languages with state and control.
- 2004 JLAP reprint is the citable archival form.
