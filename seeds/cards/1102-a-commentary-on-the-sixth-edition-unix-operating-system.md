# A Commentary on the Sixth Edition UNIX Operating System

## One-sentence takeaway

Lions' commentary made UNIX V6 readable line-by-line—the canonical teaching autopsy of a real kernel.

## Why it matters here

Model for how Broadside should read systems: primary source + guided commentary, not slides alone.

## Key ideas

- Complete annotated walkthrough of a production-scale kernel.
- Process, trap, and file-system paths explained together.
- Underground classic that trained a generation of OS engineers.
- Pairs with the Ritchie–Thompson CACM paper.

## Caveats

- Originally restricted circulation; modern reprints vary in pagination.
