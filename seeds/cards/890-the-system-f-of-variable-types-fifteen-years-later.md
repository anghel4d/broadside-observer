# The System F of Variable Types, Fifteen Years Later

## One-sentence takeaway

Retrospective that made System F the standard reference for second-order polymorphic lambda calculus.

## Why it matters here

The readable System F paper most PL people actually cite — pairs with TAPL chapters on polymorphism.

## Key ideas

- Clean presentation of System F syntax, typing, and normalization.
- Connects 1971 proof-theory origins to programming-language practice.
- Standard citation for impredicative polymorphism.
- Companion to Reynolds' type-structure and parametricity papers.
