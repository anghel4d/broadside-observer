# Probabilistic Computations: Toward a Unified Measure of Complexity

## One-sentence takeaway

Yao minimax principle: average-case deterministic complexity equals worst-case randomized complexity.

## Why it matters here

Standard tool for randomized algorithm lower bounds.

## Key ideas

- Distributional complexity equals randomized complexity.
- Lower bounds via hard distributions.
- Unifies probabilistic computation measures.
- Used in communication complexity too.

## Caveats

- Finite/zero-error variants need careful statement.
- Does not by itself yield the hard distribution.
