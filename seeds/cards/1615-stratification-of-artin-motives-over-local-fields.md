# Stratification of Artin motives over local fields

## One-sentence takeaway

Permutation motives for p-decomposition groups are Balmer-stratified with generically noetherian spectrum — tt-geometry meeting local-field arithmetic.

## Why it's lovely

Why you might love this: Artin/permutation motives, local fields, and Balmer stratification in one package. After a day of spectrum counterexamples, here is a positive stratification theorem with an arithmetic payoff for nonarchimedean local fields.

## Key ideas

- For each p-decomposition group P, DPerm(P;k) is stratified in the Balmer sense.
- Balmer spectrum generically noetherian.
- Arithmetic application to motives over nonarchimedean local fields.
- Companion tone to Nerves-of-Steel counterexamples: when stratification *does* hold.

## Caveats

Characteristic-p / local-field setting; not a general tt-stratification machine for arbitrary motives.

## Implications for Broadside

Concrete Balmer stratification in an arithmetic category — the constructive twin of spectrum-comparison drama on the same shelf.
