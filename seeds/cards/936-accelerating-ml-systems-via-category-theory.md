# Accelerating Machine Learning Systems via Category Theory: Applications to Spherical Attention for Gene Regulatory Networks

## One-sentence takeaway

Category-theory constructions are used to accelerate an ML system, with spherical attention on gene-regulatory networks as the worked non-grid geometry.

## Why it matters here

Abbott-lab systems paper sitting between NCDs and the 2026 weaves framework — same algebra, different domain. Shows the diagram/term story is not locked to Euclidean grids.

## Key ideas

- AGI 2025 proceedings Part I, pp. 1–11; DOI `10.1007/978-3-032-00686-8_1`.
- Treats architecture acceleration as a categorical construction rather than a one-off kernel.
- Spherical attention: a geometry without a grid that still has to broadcast, so the axis/weaving story still applies.
- Short conference paper; the algebra is developed more fully in Weaves 2026.
