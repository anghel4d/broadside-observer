# Dynamic Portal Occlusion for Precomputed Interactive Sound Propagation

## One-sentence takeaway

Dynamic portal occlusion on a *precomputed* wave field: open/close doors by modulating baked transfer instead of rebaking (Chaitanya/Raghuvanshi et al., arXiv 2107.11548).

## Why it matters here

Triton bakes die when a blast door moves. This 2021 arXiv (Project Acoustics authors) is the portal leftover: treat openings as dynamic operators on a static bake. Anoptic interiors must have this or 2D wave (1754).

## Key ideas

- **Portals as dynamic occlusion** on precomputed interactive sound.
- **arXiv 2107.11548** (DOI 10.48550/arXiv.2107.11548).
- **Microsoft wave-coding line.** Same authors as 2020 reciprocal coding.
- **Geometry mostly static; doors aren't.**

## Caveats

Portal model ≠ arbitrary destruction. arXiv vs TOG. Not geometric GSound path cache. Not FDTD live.
