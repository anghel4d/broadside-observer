# Solving olympiad geometry without human demonstrations

## One-sentence takeaway

AlphaGeometry: 100M synthetic theorems train a language model to propose auxiliary constructions for a symbolic DD+AR engine — 25/30 recent IMO geometry problems, near average gold.

## Why it matters here

The 2024 Nature event: olympiad geometry without human proofs. Neuro-symbolic, not a MATH increment.

## Problem

Geometry has almost no formal training data; translation into a machine language is itself the bottleneck.

## Design

Synthesize theorems and proofs across difficulty; LM proposes auxiliaries; deduction engine closes. Human-readable proofs.

## Evidence

Nature 625, 476-482 (2024). DOI 10.1038/s41586-023-06747-5. arXiv:2312.12272. 25/30 vs previous 10; IMO 2000 and 2015 geometry all solved under expert eval.

## Limitations

Plane geometry only; specialised language. Silver 2024 needed AlphaProof for the rest.
