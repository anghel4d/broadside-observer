# The generator problem for finite factors

## One-sentence takeaway

OpenAI's result family 296 (Operator algebras) claims: Proves that every type II1 factor with separable predual is generated by a single operator, equivalently by two self-adjoint operators, resolving the generator problem.

## Key ideas

- Family summary (repo): More strongly, for every irreducible inclusion $P\subset M$ of such factors, the unitaries u with $M=W^*(P,u)$ form a dense Gδ subset in the trace 2-norm topology.
- *Relative generation and the generator problem for finite factors*: We prove that every type II1 factor with separable predual is generated by one operator, equivalently by two self-adjoint operators. Combined with the established direct-integral reduction to this case, this gives an affirmative solution of the generator problem for von Neumann algebras with separable predual.
- Lean scope (lean/docs/296.md): The formalization answers the generator problem affirmatively for type $\mathrm{II}_1$ factors with separable predual: one bounded operator generates the factor under adjoints and weak-operator closure. It imposes no separability assumption on the representing Hilbert space or on the operator-norm topology.
