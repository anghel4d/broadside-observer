# On Tables of Random Numbers

## One-sentence takeaway

Kolmogorov reflects on what makes a table random—bridge to algorithmic randomness.

## Why it matters here

Companion to three-approaches; practical randomness epistemology.

## Key ideas

- Finite-sequence randomness intuitions.
- Critique of purely probabilistic accounts for individual strings.
