# The Formal Semantics of Programming Languages: An Introduction

## One-sentence takeaway

Introductory textbook covering operational, denotational, and axiomatic semantics in one course path.

## Why it matters here

Winskel is the standard teaching companion beside Gunter/Stoy for semantics courses.

## Key ideas

- IMP and related case-study languages.
- Relates SOS, denotational, and Hoare-style views.
- Domain theory at textbook depth.
- Pairs with Plotkin SOS and Scott–Strachey.
