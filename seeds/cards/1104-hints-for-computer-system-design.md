# Hints for Computer System Design

## One-sentence takeaway

Lampson's hint list: interfaces, haste, completeness, and experience-packed maxims for building systems that last.

## Why it matters here

The craft checklist for Broadside engine and tool design reviews.

## Key ideas

- Keep interfaces stable; hide implementation.
- Do one thing well; make the common case fast.
- Handle failure and partial operations explicitly.
- Experience distilled from OS/language/distributed projects.

## Caveats

- Classic; verify claims against the primary text before engineering decisions.
