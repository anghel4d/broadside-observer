# Computer Graphics: Principles and Practice

## One-sentence takeaway

The Foley–van Dam textbook canonized the graphics pipeline, clipping, illumination, and interaction for decades of engineers.

## Why it matters here

Single textbook card for the pipeline vocabulary Broadside graphics threads assume.

## Key ideas

- Standard pipeline presentation (geometry → raster).
- Clipping, transforms, shading chapters as shared language.
- Practice-oriented algorithms beside theory.
- Bridge from Sketchpad-era ideas to GPU pipelines.

## Caveats

- Edition matters (1982 vs 1990 vs later 3rd ed.).
