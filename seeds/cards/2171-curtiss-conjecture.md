## Key ideas

- *The Stable Hurewicz Image of the Sphere at Two*: We prove Curtis's conjecture: in every positive degree, the mod-two stable Hurewicz image of the sphere is spanned by the images of η, ν, σ and the Kervaire-invariant-one classes that exist. Consequently, Eccles's conjecture holds for every sphere Sn with n > 0.
