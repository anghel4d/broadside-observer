# Maass waveforms and low-lying zeros

## One-sentence takeaway

Katz-Sarnak for families of cuspidal Maass forms: orthogonal symmetry, support (-3/2,3/2) as square-free level goes to infinity, and 2-level density to pin the type when level is fixed.

## Why it's lovely

REU-era paper with Miller; the level-aspect companion of 1215.

## Problem

ILS did holomorphic forms; do Maass forms match orthogonal?

## Design

Weighted Kuznetsov / Petersson-style averages; 1- and 2-level densities.

## Evidence

In Pomerance-Rassias (eds.), Analytic Number Theory, Springer 2015, 19-56. DOI 10.1007/978-3-319-22240-0_2. arXiv:1306.5886.

## Limitations

Restricted test functions. Book chapter, not a journal.

## Implications for Broadside

See 1215; do not collapse the two.

## Bottom line

Mint the Springer chapter.
