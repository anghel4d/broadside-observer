# The Mechanical Evaluation of Expressions

## One-sentence takeaway

Introduces the SECD machine for mechanical evaluation of λ-expressions — the ancestor abstract machine.

## Why it matters here

SECD is the root abstract machine card — everything CEK/ZINC/Krivine answers to Landin.

## Key ideas

- SECD: Stack, Environment, Control, Dump.
- Lambda expressions as a programming language core.
- Closure-based evaluation model.
- Direct ancestor of ISWIM and later machines.
