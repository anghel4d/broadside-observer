# Recursive Functions of Symbolic Expressions and Their Computation by Machine, Part I

## One-sentence takeaway

Introduces LISP: symbolic expressions, recursive functions, and the eval meta-circular interpreter.

## Why it matters here

The LISP paper — obligatory root for Scheme, macros, and homoiconic tool languages.

## Key ideas

- S-expressions and recursive list processing.
- eval/apply as a definitional interpreter.
- Automatic memory management (garbage collection).
- Conditional expressions and recursive function definitions.
