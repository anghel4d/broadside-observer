# The Computer and the Brain

## One-sentence takeaway

Von Neumann compares digital architectures with neural wetware—precision, parallelism, and memory tradeoffs.

## Why it matters here

Classic computer↔brain essay; pairs with McCulloch–Pitts and Wiener.

## Key ideas

- Digital vs analog mechanisms in brain.
- Memory and precision arguments.
- Unfinished Silliman Lectures.
