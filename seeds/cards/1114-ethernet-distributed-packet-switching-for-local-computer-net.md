# Ethernet: Distributed Packet Switching for Local Computer Networks

## One-sentence takeaway

Ethernet shows CSMA/CD packet switching on a shared cable—the LAN that ate the world.

## Why it matters here

Link-layer assumptions under every Broadside lab and office deploy.

## Key ideas

- Best-effort datagrams on a broadcast medium.
- Carrier sense / collision detection.
- Simple controllers, distributed arbitration.
- From Alto/PARC to IEEE 802.3 lineage.

## Caveats

- Classic; verify claims against the primary text before engineering decisions.
