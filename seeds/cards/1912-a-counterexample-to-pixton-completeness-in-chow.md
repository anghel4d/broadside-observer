# A counterexample to Pixton completeness in Chow

## One-sentence takeaway

OpenAI's result family 053 (Algebraic and complex geometry) claims: Constructs a tautological relation on a moduli space of stable pointed curves that vanishes in rational Chow, hence in rational cohomology, but lies outside Pixton's original relation span.

## Key ideas

- Family summary (repo): This disproves the Chow and rational-cohomological forms of his original completeness conjecture.
- *A high-arity counterexample to Pixton completeness in Chow*: We disprove the Chow and rational-cohomological forms of Pixton's completeness conjecture for his original relation system. We construct a formal tautological class outside the original Pixton relation span whose image is zero in the Chow ring and in rational cohomology. The example has genus $10^{60}$ and $3\binom{10^{60}}3$ markings.
