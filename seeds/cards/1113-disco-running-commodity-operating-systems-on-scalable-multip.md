# Disco: Running Commodity Operating Systems on Scalable Multiprocessors

## One-sentence takeaway

Disco virtualizes scalable multiprocessors so commodity OSes see a familiar machine—direct precursor thinking to VMware/Xen.

## Why it matters here

NUMA virtualization history for multi-socket engine hosts.

## Key ideas

- VMs as a scalability compatibility layer.
- Share-memory multiprocessor virtualization.
- Commodity OS reuse on novel hardware.
- Stanford lineage into VMware.
