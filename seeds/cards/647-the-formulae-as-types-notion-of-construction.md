# The Formulae-as-Types Notion of Construction

## One-sentence takeaway

Makes explicit the formulae-as-types correspondence between natural deduction and typed lambda terms.

## Why it matters here

The Howard half of Curry–Howard — required reading for dependent types, proof assistants, and typed-IR design.

## Key ideas

- Proofs as programs / formulae as types.
- Connects intuitionistic natural deduction to simply typed lambda calculus.
- Written 1969; published 1980 in the Curry festschrift.
- Ancestor of Martin-Löf type theory presentations.
