# Donovan's conjecture over fields and complete mixed-characteristic DVRs

## One-sentence takeaway

OpenAI's result family 203 (Algebra) claims: Proves Donovan's conjecture: over each fixed algebraically closed field of characteristic p, blocks of finite groups with bounded defect-group order have only finitely many Morita-equivalence classes, for every prime p.

## Key ideas

- Family summary (repo): Also proves the integral form over each fixed complete mixed-characteristic discrete valuation ring with algebraically closed residue field.
- *Donovan's Conjecture over Algebraically Closed Fields*: We prove Donovan's conjecture over every algebraically closed field K of characteristic p > 0. For each fixed K and bound on defect-group order, blocks of finite groups represent only finitely many K-linear Morita equivalence classes. The defect groups need not be abelian, and the result includes p = 2.
- *Integral Donovan Finiteness over Witt Vectors*: We prove integral Donovan finiteness: for every prime p and positive integer M, the blocks of all finite groups with defect groups of order at most M have only finitely many Morita equivalence classes over $W(\overline{\mathbb F}_p)$. The defect groups need not be abelian, and the result includes p = 2. The same bounded-defect finiteness holds over each fixed complete discrete valuation ring of characteristic zero with algebraically closed residue field of characteristic p, including ramified rings.
