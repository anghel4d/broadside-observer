# The Preparation of Programs for an Electronic Digital Computer

## One-sentence takeaway

Wilkes–Wheeler–Gill document EDSAC programming and the subroutine library—software as reusable artifact.

## Why it matters here

First true programming textbook; subroutine idea becomes universal.

## Key ideas

- EDSAC order code.
- Closed subroutine library.
- Practical programming discipline 1951.

## Caveats

- Classic; read primary text before overclaiming modern interpretations.
