# Modularity and effective Mordell I

## One-sentence takeaway

Effective Faltings for curves that map to Hilbert modular stacks over odd-degree totally real fields, via effective Shafarevich for GL_2-type abelian varieties over those fields — including height bounds on C_a: x^6+4y^3=a^2.

## Why it's lovely

Unconditional effectivity in a modular family. Over Q-bar every hyperbolic hyperelliptic curve has an etale cover dominating C_1.

## Problem

Faltings is ineffective. Need a modularity handle.

## Design

Effective Shafarevich for GL_2-type over odd-degree totally real K, then pull back to the curves.

## Evidence

arXiv:2109.07917, v2 24 Nov 2021.

## Limitations

Odd-degree totally real. Part I; no Part II on arXiv as of ingest.

## Implications for Broadside

The unconditional special case 1200 dreamed of. See 1203.
