# Creating Interactive Virtual Acoustic Environments

## One-sentence takeaway

Savioja/Huopaniemi/Lokki/Väänänen 1999 JAES: the classic interactive virtual-acoustic *pipeline* (model, spatialize, binaural/VBAP) from Helsinki.
