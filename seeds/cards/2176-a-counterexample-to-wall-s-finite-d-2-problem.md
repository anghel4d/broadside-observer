# A counterexample to Wall's finite D(2) problem

## One-sentence takeaway

OpenAI's result family 321 (Topology) claims: Constructs a finite connected three-dimensional CW complex whose universal cover has no integral homology above degree two and whose third cohomology vanishes for every local coefficient module, but which has no finite two-dimensional homotopy model.

## Key ideas

- Family summary (repo): This disproves Wall's finite D(2) conjecture; the example has infinite fundamental group.
- *A Counterexample to Wall's D(2) Problem*: We give a negative answer to Wall's finite $D(2)$ problem. We construct a finite connected three-dimensional CW complex satisfying the $D(2)$ finiteness condition but not homotopy equivalent to any finite CW complex of dimension at most two. The example has infinite fundamental group.
