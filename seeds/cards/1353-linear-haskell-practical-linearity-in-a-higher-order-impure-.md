## One-sentence takeaway

Linear Haskell adds practical linear types to GHC for resource-aware APIs.

## Why it matters here

Brings linear types to a mainstream FP language—complements Rust's affinity story.

## Key ideas

- Linear function arrows.
- Multiplicities and laziness.
- Interop with existing Haskell.
