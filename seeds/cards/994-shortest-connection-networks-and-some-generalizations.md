# Shortest Connection Networks and Some Generalizations

## One-sentence takeaway

Prim’s MST grows a single tree by lightest outgoing edge.

## Why it matters here

Dense-graph MST workhorse; Jarník–Prim–Dijkstra family kinship.

## Key ideas

- Cut-property greedy growth.
- Heap implementations.
- Structure related to Dijkstra.
- Practical with adjacency matrices.

## Caveats

- Jarník 1930 priority historically.
- Heap choice dominates asymptotics.
