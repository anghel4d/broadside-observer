# Weaves, Wires, and Morphisms: Formalizing and Implementing the Algebra of Deep Learning

## One-sentence takeaway

A categorical algebra for deep-learning architectures: broadcasting is an axis-stride / array-broadcasted category `[B;A]` over BorelStoch with affine indexes, Para hides weights, and weaves distinguish tiled vs target axes — implemented in mirrored Python (`pyncd`) and TypeScript (`tsncd`), with DeepSeek-V3 as the worked example.

## Why it matters here

This is the typed-array / notation-as-IR paper Broadside has been missing next to rank polymorphism. Neural circuit diagrams are a compositional blueprint language (human diagrams ↔ machine terms) in the same spirit as ano: an architecture is a term, not a pile of `view`/`expand` comments. Dual Python/TypeScript implementations are the kind of portable IR Anoptic wants if GRID COMMAND ever grows a typed tensor surface for agents.

## Key ideas

- Ad-hoc notation and pseudocode fail on *nonlinear broadcasting* and on the component-vs-composed-model relationship. The fix is a constructed term system with sound interpretation into a mathematical category.
- **Axis-stride** and **array-broadcasted** categories: objects carry axes; morphisms reindex. The structured form is `[B;A]` — BorelStoch (`B`, standard Borel spaces and measurable Markov kernels) for datatypes/probability, affine indexing category `A` for strides.
- **Weaves** (and integrated reindexings) separate *tiled* axes from *target* axes, which is how FlashAttention-style tiling and convolution/MoE broadcasting become the same algebra rather than special cases.
- **Para(`[B;A]`)** (Fong–Spivak–Tuyéras) wraps the symmetric monoidal category so learned weights are hidden parameters rather than extra wires.
- Operations recovered uniformly: Einstein (einsum) products, dropout/elementwise, embeddings, MoE, convolution.
- **DeepSeek-V3** is the large worked example (MLA + DeepSeekMoE expressed as terms). The stack then: algebraic construction → hypergraph ↔ diagram → PyTorch compilation (`pyncd`) and diagram rendering (`tsncd`).
- Continues Neural Circuit Diagrams (TMLR 2024) and *FlashAttention on a Napkin* (TMLR 2025): diagrams that are also a calculus.

## Caveats

- Framework paper: the DeepSeek-V3 encoding is a representation, not a training recipe or a kernel.
- BorelStoch + affine indexes are a *chosen* semantic base (probability + strided arrays); differentiability is explicitly out of scope here (see Cruttwell/Para/lenses for gradient).
- Dual-language implementations demonstrate universality of the term system, not production compiler quality.
- 35-item bibliography mixes software repos (pyncd/tsncd: GitHub only, no cards) with papers.
