# Towards a Theory of Type Structure

## One-sentence takeaway

Independently invents second-order polymorphic lambda calculus (System F) as a typed theory of abstraction.

## Why it matters here

Reynolds System F is the type-theoretic backbone for parametric polymorphism in ano APIs and for reading Wadler free theorems.

## Key ideas

- Polymorphic types as abstraction over type structure, not merely over values.
- Second-order lambda calculus as a foundation for typed programming.
- Sets up Reynolds' later parametricity reading of polymorphism.
- Twin discovery with Girard's System F from proof theory.
