# A Protocol for Packet Network Intercommunication

## One-sentence takeaway

Cerf–Kahn define internetworking: gateways, common host protocol, and process-level communication across heterogeneous packet nets.

## Why it matters here

The Internet's founding protocol paper—context for every game-net and tool RPC stack.

## Key ideas

- Gateway-connected heterogeneous networks.
- Host-to-host protocol with ports/processes.
- Addressing and fragmentation across nets.
- Ancestor of TCP/IP split (RFC 791/793).

## Caveats

- Classic; verify claims against the primary text before engineering decisions.
