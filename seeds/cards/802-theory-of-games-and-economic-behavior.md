# Theory of Games and Economic Behavior

## One-sentence takeaway

Von Neumann–Morgenstern axiomatize utility and expand game theory into a full mathematical economics of strategy.

## Why it matters here

Formal utility + games underpin agent objectives and multi-agent simulation.

## Key ideas

- vNM utility axioms.
- Zero-sum and wider game forms.
- Coalition/extensive-form beginnings.

## Caveats

- Classic; read primary text before overclaiming modern interpretations.
