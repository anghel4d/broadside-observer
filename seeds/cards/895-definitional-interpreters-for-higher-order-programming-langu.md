# Definitional Interpreters for Higher-Order Programming Languages

## One-sentence takeaway

Shows how to derive interpreters by successive definitional transformations, introducing defunctionalization.

## Why it matters here

Still the masterclass in turning language definitions into executable interpreters — the method behind many ano semantics sketches.

## Key ideas

- Definitional interpreters as a method, not a one-off interpreter.
- CPS transformation to make control explicit.
- Defunctionalization of higher-order functions into first-order apply.
- Bridge from denotational thinking to operational implementability.
