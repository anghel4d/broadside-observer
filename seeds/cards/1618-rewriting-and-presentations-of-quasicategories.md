# Rewriting and presentations of quasicategories

## One-sentence takeaway

Convergent rewrite presentations of ordinary categories lift to coherent presentations of the corresponding quasicategory — rewriting theory as ∞-coherence.

## Why it's lovely

Why you might love this: critical-pair / convergent rewriting is how you prove coherence classically; Henry shows the same toolkit speaks quasicategories. String-diagram critical pairs (1027) meet (∞,1)-presentations.

## Key ideas

- Start from a category with a convergent rewrite-system presentation.
- The associated weak (∞,1)-category admits a coherent presentation inherited from the rewrite system.
- Rewriting methods → coherence theorems at quasicategory level.
- Bridge between combinatorial rewriting and higher-categorical presentations.

## Caveats

Needs a convergent presentation of the 1-category first; not automatic for arbitrary quasicategories.

## Implications for Broadside

Directly adjacent to ano/language coherence instincts and the string-diagram critical-pair card (1027): rewrite systems as a path to higher coherence.
