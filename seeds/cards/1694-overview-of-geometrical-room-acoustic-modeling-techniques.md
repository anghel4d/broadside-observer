# Overview of geometrical room acoustic modeling techniques

## One-sentence takeaway

Savioja & Svensson 2015 JASA: the definitive survey of geometrical room-acoustic modeling — paths, surfaces, diffraction, auralization, radiosity.

## Why it matters here

Before minting another tracer, read this. It classifies image source / ray / beam / radiosity / diffraction (UTD vs BTM) and what each cannot do. The geometric-acoustics syllabus card for Anoptic.

## Key ideas

- **Taxonomy.** Path-based vs surface-based; specular vs diffuse vs diffracted.
- **Diffraction review.** UTD vs BTMS vs energy-based approximations.
- **Auralization practice.** How modeling choices hit the ear.
- **JASA 2015.** Harvest: 'definitive modern survey.'

## Caveats

Survey cutoff 2015 (pre-BDPT audio 2016, pre-diffraction pathfinding 2021, pre-differentiable 2025). Not a wave-methods survey (that's FDTD/FVTD papers).
