# The product rule in Goodwillie calculus

## One-sentence takeaway

Goodwillie derivatives satisfy a product rule: for C with Sp-stabilization, the derivatives functor turns products into Day convolution of spectra sequences.

## Why it's lovely

Why you might love this: Goodwillie calculus is the Taylor tower for functors, and a product rule is the sort of thing you expect from any honest derivative — but proving it at the ∞-categorical level is non-trivial. Flagged as a Sep-3 Curiosity near-keeper; finally minted. Pure homotopy-calculus sparkle.

## Key ideas

- Differentiable ∞-categories whose stabilization is Sp.
- Derivatives functor ∂_* on finitary functors.
- Product rule: derivatives of a product become a Day-type convolution of spectra sequences.
- Closes a folklore gap in modern Goodwillie calculus.

## Caveats

Assumes Sp-stabilization; not the most general differentiable setting. Technique paper more than an application showcase.
