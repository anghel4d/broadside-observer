# Relativizations of the P =? NP Question

## One-sentence takeaway

Oracle worlds exist with P=NP and with P≠NP — relativizing proofs cannot settle P vs NP.

## Why it matters here

Explains why many proof styles fail; shapes later non-relativizing methods.

## Key ideas

- Oracles A,B with opposite P vs NP answers.
- Barrier for diagonalization-style proofs.
- Motivates circuit/PCP techniques.
- Structural-complexity warning.

## Caveats

- Does not say P vs NP is independent.
- Later barriers refine the story.
