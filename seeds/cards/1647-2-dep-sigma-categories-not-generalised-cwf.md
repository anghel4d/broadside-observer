# (2-dep,$Σ$)-categories are not generalised categories with families

## One-sentence takeaway

(2-dep, Σ)-categories — a natural 2-dimensional dependent-type doctrine — are not generalised categories with families.

## Why it's lovely

Why you might love this: the zoo of "categorical dependent type theory" doctrines keeps growing (CwF, comprehension categories, natural models, generalised CwF…). Gambarte shows a clean 2-dimensional candidate sits outside the generalised-CwF biequivalence class. Negative results that redraw the map of the zoo are peak curiosity.

## Key ideas

- Generalised CwF (Coraglia–Emmenegger) biequivalent to comprehension categories.
- (2-dep, Σ)-categories as a 2-dimensional dependent-typing doctrine.
- They are not generalised CwF — the doctrines genuinely diverge.
- Useful boundary marker for anyone formalising dependent types categorically (ano / proof assistants).

## Caveats

Does not propose a "best" doctrine; only separates two candidates. Read as a map-correction, not a compiler recipe.
