# FrontierMath: A Benchmark for Evaluating Advanced Mathematical Reasoning in AI

## One-sentence takeaway

Epoch AI's private, expert-authored research-grade math benchmark: leading 2024 models (o1-preview, GPT-4o, Claude 3.5, Gemini 1.5 Pro) all below 2% — the anti-GSM8K.

## Why it matters here

User named FrontierMath as the benchmark paper to mint. It is the eval the later IMO/o-series events are measured against, not a +2% MATH paper.

## Problem

MATH and GSM8K saturated; contamination is rife. Need unpublished, specialist problems that take humans hours or days.

## Design

Hundreds of new problems across number theory, algebraic geometry, etc., peer-reviewed, held private. Tiers 1-4 plus a later Open Problems track.

## Evidence

arXiv:2411.04872 (Nov 2024). epoch.ai/frontiermath. Commissioned in part by OpenAI (disclosed).

## Limitations

Not a solve paper. Private labels. OpenAI ownership of a slice is a contamination footnote. Putnam-Bench / MiniF2F are benches, not minted.

## Implications for Broadside

The eval card for the AI-math shelf. See o1 1228 and IMO gold 1229/1230 as the systems that later moved the needle.

## Bottom line

Mint FrontierMath. Skip Putnam-Bench and MiniF2F as standalone benches.
