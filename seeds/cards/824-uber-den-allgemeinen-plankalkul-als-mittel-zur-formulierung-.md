# Über den allgemeinen Plankalkül als Mittel zur Formulierung schematisch-kombinativer Aufgaben

## One-sentence takeaway

Zuse's Plankalkül is an early high-level algorithmic language—predicates, types, and chess examples before Fortran.

## Why it matters here

Shows HLLs are not a 1957 invention; European parallel to US automatic programming.

## Key ideas

- Plankalkül notation for algorithms.
- Data structures / predicates.
- Designed ~1945; publication delayed by war.
- Zuse Z3 context.

## Caveats

- Design 1945; publications staggered—cite carefully.
