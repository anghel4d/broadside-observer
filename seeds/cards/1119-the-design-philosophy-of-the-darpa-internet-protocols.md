# The Design Philosophy of the DARPA Internet Protocols

## One-sentence takeaway

Clark explains Internet priorities: multiplexed utilization, survivability, and the fate-sharing / soft-state philosophy.

## Why it matters here

Why the Internet looks like it does—and which goals to copy or reject in game nets.

## Key ideas

- Fundamental goal: multiplexed interconnection of networks.
- Second-level goals ranked (survivability, types of service, …).
- Fate sharing and soft state.
- End-to-end arguments in architectural context.

- Classic; verify claims against the primary text before engineering decisions.
