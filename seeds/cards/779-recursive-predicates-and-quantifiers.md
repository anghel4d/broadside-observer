# Recursive predicates and quantifiers

## One-sentence takeaway

Kleene develops the arithmetical hierarchy of predicates—complexity of definability before runtime complexity.

## Why it matters here

Ancestor of descriptive complexity and 'how hard to define' thinking in Broadside.

## Key ideas

- Σ⁰ₙ / Π⁰ₙ hierarchy.
- Quantifier alternations over recursive predicates.
- Normal form theorems.

## Caveats

- Classic; read primary text before overclaiming modern interpretations.
