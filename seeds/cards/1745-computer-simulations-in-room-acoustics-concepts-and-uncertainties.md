# Computer simulations in room acoustics: Concepts and uncertainties

## One-sentence takeaway

Vorländer 2013 JASA: concepts and *uncertainties* in room-acoustic computer simulation — what the numbers don't mean.

## Why it matters here

The humility card. Input absorption, scattering (1751), and discretization dominate error. Anoptic should treat T30 from a tracer as ± a lot unless BRAS-validated. Harvest interactive-acoustics / room-acoustics, 155 cites.

## Key ideas

- **Uncertainty budget.** Geometry, materials, method class (GA vs wave).
- **JASA 2013.** Concepts, not a new solver.
- **Pairs with 2019 round robin.**
- **Simulation vs auralization** errors are different.

## Caveats

Survey/position. 2013. Doesn't implement a method. Not a remint of 1989 hybrid.
