# A Hidden Surface Algorithm for Computer Generated Halftone Pictures

## One-sentence takeaway

Warnock's area subdivision algorithm attacks hidden surfaces by recursive screen-space subdivision until regions are trivial.

## Why it matters here

Divide-and-conquer visibility—ancestor rhetoric for hierarchical Z and GPU-driven culling (161, 730).

## Key ideas

- Recursive screen-space subdivision.
- Trivial accept/reject of regions.
- Early Utah graphics school classic.
- Conceptual kin to later hierarchical visibility.

## Caveats

- Classic; verify claims against the primary text before engineering decisions.
