# On the Structure of Polynomial Time Reducibility

## One-sentence takeaway

Ladner’s theorem: if P≠NP then there exist NP-intermediate problems.

## Why it matters here

Warns that NP-hard vs P is not the only landscape.

## Key ideas

- Diagonalization construction of intermediate languages.
- Rich degree structure inside NP if P≠NP.
- NP-intermediate sets exist unless P=NP.
- Structural complexity classic.

## Caveats

- Non-constructive for natural problems.
- Graph isomorphism later quasi-P.
