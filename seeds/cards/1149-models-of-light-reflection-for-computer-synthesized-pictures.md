# Models of Light Reflection for Computer Synthesized Pictures

## One-sentence takeaway

Blinn popularizes practical reflection models (including Blinn–Phong style halfway-vector shading) for synthetic images.

## Why it matters here

Shading model ancestor under Disney PBR (424) and every engine light loop.

## Key ideas

- Phong-like models adapted for efficient CG.
- Halfway-vector formulation influence.
- Empirical models suitable for 1970s hardware.
- Pairs with Blinn–Newell environment mapping.

## Caveats

- Classic; verify claims against the primary text before engineering decisions.
