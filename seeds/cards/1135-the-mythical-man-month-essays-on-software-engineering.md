# The Mythical Man-Month: Essays on Software Engineering

## One-sentence takeaway

Brooks: adding people to a late project makes it later; conceptual integrity beats committee design.

## Why it matters here

Project-runtime wisdom for Broadside/Anoptic staffing and scope control.

## Key ideas

- Mythical man-month / Brooks's law.
- Conceptual integrity and the surgical team.
- Second-system effect.
- Plan to throw one away (with later caveats in anniversary ed.).

## Caveats

- Essays rooted in OS/360 experience; anniversary edition revises some advice.
