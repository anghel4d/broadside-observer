# Introduction to Automata Theory, Languages, and Computation

## One-sentence takeaway

Classic Hopcroft–Ullman textbook that standardized automata and complexity pedagogy.

## Why it matters here

Reference spine for NFA/DFA/CFG/TM material across Broadside foundations.

## Key ideas

- Unified automata, grammars, decidability, complexity.
- Standard constructions and reductions.
- Bridges language theory to algorithmics.
- One card for the book.

## Caveats

- Editions differ (1979 vs later HMU).
- Not a substitute for primary theorem papers.
