# Intuitionistic Unitary Linear Logic: A Proof-Theoretical Approach to Purely Quantum Higher-Order

## One-sentence takeaway

A proof theory for intuitionistic unitary linear logic that can host non-causal higher-order quantum processes such as the quantum switch.

## Why it's lovely

Why you might love this: ordinary quantum circuits are causal; the quantum switch is not. Lamiroy–Valiron–Vilmart give a purely proof-theoretic unitary linear logic that aims at higher-order quantum without smuggling causality back in. Diagrammatic / linear-logic sparkle with a real physics target — and a sideways glance at Broadside's 'everything is a plugin' harness mindset.

## Key ideas

- Circuit model cannot represent non-causal higher-order processes (quantum switch).
- Intuitionistic unitary linear logic as a proof theory for purely quantum higher-order.
- Keeps unitarity as a first-class constraint rather than a semantic afterthought.
- Sits with linear-logic / diagrammatic / ZX-adjacent cards without being a ZX paper.

## Caveats

Proof-theoretic proposal — not a drop-in quantum compiler. Compare carefully to existing process theories and categorical quantum mechanics accounts.
