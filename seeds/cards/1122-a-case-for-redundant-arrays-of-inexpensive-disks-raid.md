# A Case for Redundant Arrays of Inexpensive Disks (RAID)

## One-sentence takeaway

RAID argues arrays of cheap disks can beat exotic drives on performance and reliability via striping and redundancy levels.

## Why it matters here

Reliability/performance taxonomy for any Broadside artifact store or replay archive.

## Key ideas

- RAID levels 1–5 taxonomy.
- Striping for throughput.
- Redundancy for mean-time-to-data-loss.
- Inexpensive disks as the scaling path.

## Caveats

- Classic; verify claims against the primary text before engineering decisions.
