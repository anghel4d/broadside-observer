# Computing Machine for the Solution of Large Systems of Linear Algebraic Equations

## One-sentence takeaway

Atanasoff's August 1940 manuscript specifies an electronic binary machine that solves large linear systems by mechanized elimination — the ABC design.

## Why it matters here

Electronic digital arithmetic for linear algebra predates the EDVAC draft. Engine numerics sit on that hardware idea, whatever later courtroom priority fights said.

## Key ideas

- Target problems: least squares, multiple correlation, circuit analysis, elasticity, quantum mechanics — all large \(Ax=b\).
- Arithmetic is binary; logic is electronic; coefficients live on a rotating drum / capacitor memory.
- Gaussian elimination is the algorithm, carried out by a special-purpose digital data path rather than a stored-program general computer.
- Iowa State catalog item atanasoff143 (31 pages, RS 13/20/51) is the archival source.
- Honeywell v. Sperry Rand later treated this manuscript as evidence of prior electronic digital technique.
