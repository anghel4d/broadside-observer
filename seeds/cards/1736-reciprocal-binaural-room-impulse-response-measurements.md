# Reciprocal binaural room impulse response measurements

## One-sentence takeaway

Reciprocal BRIR measurement: swap loudspeaker/mic using reciprocity to speed binaural room IR campaigns (Klein/Pollow/Fels/Vorländer JASA 2012).

## Why it matters here

Measuring BRIRs for every head pose is brutal. Reciprocity (source at ear, mics in room) is a lab trick Anoptic won't run, but it explains HUTUBS/SONICOM acquisition papers. Harvest BRIR/HRTF/SH.

## Key ideas

- **Acoustic reciprocity.** Ear↔room swap under linear acoustics.
- **JASA 2012.** DOI 10.1121/1.4755015; Semantic Scholar URL in harvest.
- **Measured BRIR.** Not simulated Wendt 2014.
- **Spherical-harmonics tagged** — spatial sampling of the room field.

## Caveats

Lab method, not a renderer. Reciprocity assumes LTI air (no wind, no nonlinear PA). Not dry HRTF CIPIC.
