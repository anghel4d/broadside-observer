# A Perceptual Evaluation of Individual and Non-Individual HRTFs: A Case Study of the SADIE II Database

## One-sentence takeaway

SADIE II: high-resolution measured HRTFs plus a perceptual test of individual vs generic sets — the modern 'is personalization worth it' case study.

## Why it matters here

York SADIE is what Ambisonic/binaural research (MagLS, I3DA) actually convolves. Armstrong et al. 2018 Applied Sciences evaluates individual vs generic localization/timbre. Anoptic VR: SADIE as the high-res measured set; CIPIC as anthropometry; KEMAR as the tiny fallback.

## Key ideas

- **SADIE II database.** Dense spatial HRTFs, multiple subjects, research-friendly.
- **Individual vs generic.** Perceptual listening, not just spectral L2.
- **Applied Sciences 2018 OA.** Easy to link; harvest quality 9.
- **Feeds MagLS papers** that need a real HRTF, not a spherical-head model.

## Caveats

Still measured far-field HRTF, not a BRIR (room). Subject count ≪ HUTUBS/SONICOM. Headphone EQ still required (Møller).
