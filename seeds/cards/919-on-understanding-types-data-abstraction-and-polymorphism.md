# On Understanding Types, Data Abstraction, and Polymorphism

## One-sentence takeaway

Landmark survey clarifying varieties of polymorphism and the type structure of data abstraction.

## Why it matters here

The Cardelli–Wegner map of polymorphism — still the taxonomy people mean by universal vs ad-hoc.

## Key ideas

- Universal (parametric/inclusion) vs ad-hoc polymorphism.
- Type systems for abstract data types and objects.
- Functors/existentials as abstraction mechanisms.
- Bridge from Milner polymorphism to object-oriented type theory.
