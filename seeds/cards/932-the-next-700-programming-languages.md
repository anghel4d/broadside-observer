# The Next 700 Programming Languages

## One-sentence takeaway

Argues for a family of languages (ISWIM) sharing a λ-calculus core rather than endless one-off syntaxes.

## Why it matters here

The language-design manifesto card — still the right attitude for ano DSL proliferation.

## Key ideas

- ISWIM: If You See What I Mean.
- Shared semantic core with sugared surface languages.
- Offside rule and applicative-style programming.
- Frames PL design as family resemblance, not monoliths.
