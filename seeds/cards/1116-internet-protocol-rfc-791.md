# Internet Protocol (RFC 791)

## One-sentence takeaway

RFC 791 standardizes IPv4: unreliable datagram service, addressing, and fragmentation—the thin waist of the Internet.

## Why it matters here

IP is the substrate under Broadside networking and any GRID COMMAND WAN assumption.

## Key ideas

- Best-effort connectionless datagrams.
- IP addressing and header format.
- Fragmentation/reassembly.
- Strict layering under TCP/UDP.

## Caveats

- Classic; verify claims against the primary text before engineering decisions.
