# The average number of integral points on elliptic curves is bounded

## One-sentence takeaway

When Weierstrass models E_{A,B}/Q are ordered by naive height, the average of #E(Z) is < 66 (at most 8/9 on the minimalist conjecture) — the Harvard senior thesis / Fay Prize paper, and the last solo journal-track result he delayed on purpose.

## Why it's lovely

Silverman / Helfgott / Helfgott-Venkatesh sphere-packing plus Bhargava-Shankar Selmer averages plus local heights for "most" curves. Same method on y^2=x^3+B, y^2=x^3+Ax, y^2=x^3-D^2 x.

## Problem

How many integral points does a random elliptic curve have?

## Design

Small / medium (spherical codes) / large (height gap) split; average 2^{rk}.

## Evidence

arXiv:1412.1047, v3 Feb 2015. Senior thesis "The average elliptic curve has few integral points" is this paper — do not remint the thesis.

## Limitations

Quasiminimal Weierstrass models, not everywhere-integral Neron models. First moment only (see 1209).

## Implications for Broadside

The origin of the Alpoge integral-points spine.

## Bottom line

Mint 1412.1047; skip the thesis PDF.
