# Eine Interpretation des intuitionistischen Aussagenkalküls

## One-sentence takeaway

Gödel embeds intuitionistic propositional logic into modal logic S4—early bridge among constructive, classical, and modal views.

## Why it matters here

Constructive logic matters for type theory and effects; this is an early precision tool.

## Key ideas

- Translation into S4.
- Shows intuitionistic logic ≠ classical with a modal gloss.
- Part of 1933 constructive suite.

## Caveats

- Classic; read primary text before overclaiming modern interpretations.
