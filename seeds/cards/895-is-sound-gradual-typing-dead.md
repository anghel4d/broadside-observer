# Is Sound Gradual Typing Dead?

## One-sentence takeaway

Shows sound gradual typing can incur catastrophic overhead on realistic programs.

## Why it matters here

Forces the performance–soundness tradeoff into real language design conversations.

## Key ideas

- Performance lattice over typed/untyped mixes.
- Pathological boundary costs.
- Motivates transient and compiler work.

## Caveats

- Part of the gradual/refinement type-safety shelf.
- Check mechanization status in follow-ons.
