# A psychophysical evaluation of near-field head-related transfer functions synthesized using a distance variation function

## One-sentence takeaway

Distance Variation Function (DVF): synthesize near-field HRTFs from a far-field measurement by a distance-dependent filter, then psychophysically test it.

## Why it matters here

You will not measure every distance. Kan/Jin/van Schaik 2009 JASA take far-field HRTFs, apply a DVF, and ask listeners if nearby sources work. Anoptic cheap near-field: DVF on CIPIC/SADIE, validate against Brungart, don't pretend it's a measured 0.2 m grid.

## Key ideas

- **DVF model.** Analytic-ish distance filter on top of far-field HRTF.
- **Psychophysics.** Not only spectral plots — localization/distance ratings.
- **JASA 2009.** Harvest near-field / ITD-ILD.
- **Modeled, not measured near-field.** Contrast Brungart 1999 measurements.

## Caveats

Spherical-head-ish assumptions; pinna near-field is messier. ~35 cites — useful method, not a database. Forced model vs unforced measurement.
