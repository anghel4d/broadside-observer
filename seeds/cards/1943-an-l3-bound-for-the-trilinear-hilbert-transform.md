## One-sentence takeaway

OpenAI's result family 086 (Real and complex analysis) claims: Proves that the principal-value trilinear Hilbert transform with shifts $x-t$, $x-2t$, $x-3t$ is bounded from $L^3(\mathbb R)^3$ to $L^1(\mathbb R)$.
