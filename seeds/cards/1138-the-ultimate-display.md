# The Ultimate Display

## One-sentence takeaway

Sutherland's ultimate display vision: a room where matter is controllable—the charter essay for VR/AR.

## Why it matters here

Aspirational north star for immersive Anoptic/GRID visualization.

## Key ideas

- Display as a window into a mathematical wonderland.
- Kinesthetic as well as visual coupling.
- Program as laws of a synthetic universe.
- Direct ancestor rhetoric of HMD work.
