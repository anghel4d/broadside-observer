# A Mathematical Theory of Communication

## One-sentence takeaway

Entropy, mutual information, and channel capacity founded modern information theory.

## Why it matters here

Baseline language for compression, coding, and uncertainty in Anoptic telemetry and agent channels.

## Key ideas

- Source and channel coding theorems as asymptotic rate limits.
- Entropy as expected self-information; mutual information as channel figure of merit.
- Noisy-channel coding theorem: reliable communication below capacity.
- Vocabulary later operationalized by Huffman, LZ, and Hamming.

## Caveats

- Asymptotic; finite-blocklength refinements came later.
- Does not itself give practical codes.
