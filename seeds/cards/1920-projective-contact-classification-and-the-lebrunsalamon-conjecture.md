## Key ideas

- Family summary (repo): It also proves contact-Fano homogeneity and classifies smooth connected complex projective contact manifolds of complex dimension at least three: those with $b_2=1$ are adjoint varieties with their canonical contact structures, while those with $b_2\ge2$ have underlying manifold $\mathbb P(T^*Z)$ for a smooth projective variety Z.
- *Contact Fano manifolds and the LeBrun–Salamon conjecture*: We resolve the contact-Fano homogeneity conjecture and the Riemannian LeBrun–Salamon conjecture positively. Consequently, every closed connected smooth positive quaternionic-Kähler manifold of real dimension $4m\geq8$ is homothetic to a compact symmetric Wolf space.
