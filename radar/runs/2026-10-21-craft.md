## Keepers
- 2371 Wide SOBB Hierarchies for Ray Tracing of Dynamic Scenes — Káčerik, Hlavová, Bittner (CGF 45(7), doi 10.1111/cgf.70643): compressed 8-wide BVH with a shared skewed-OBB basis per node, secondary rays 1.07–1.6× faster than the AABB BVH8 (about 10× on Sheep), and a refit by geometry about 1.45× the AABB refit cost (graphics, 8).
- 2372 Refit Always—Precomputed Ensembles of BVHs for Parametric Animations — Dolp, Thiery, Dachsbacher (CGF 45(7), doi 10.1111/cgf.70645): a MILP-selected set of BVH topologies over the pose space plus a tiny MLP selector, so animated characters always refit; 1.25× lower SAH and 2.4× faster than an LBVH rebuild each frame, about H-PLOC quality (graphics, 8).
- 2373 Real-Time Collision Handling for SDFs via Caching and Importance Sampling — Giles, Andrews (PG 2026, doi 10.2312/pg.20261059): softmax-plus-repulsion objective, temporal contact cache, curvature seeds and Nelder–Mead; 16k cylinders in 2.2 ms collision time; open C++/WASM code (engines, 8).
- 2374 Billions of triangles redux — Kapoulkine (zeux.io, 30 Sep): clustered-LOD lessons from NVIDIA's Zorah scene: error clamped to triangle area (geometry pool 996 → 678 MB), border dilation for foliage, meshlet codec at about 6.5 bits per triangle, meshoptimizer 1.3 (graphics, 9).
- 2375 Scrambled Multiply-with-Carry PRNGs — Vigna (arXiv 2610.27313): structural lattice limitation of every MWC generator, XOR-with-carry scrambler, recommends MWC192X at about 0.52 ns per 64-bit output with jump functions (systems, 7).
