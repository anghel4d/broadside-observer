## Keepers
- 2394 Faster than earcut: triangulating 3.7M map polygons in 489 ms — Evgen Bodunov (Globus Software blog, 16 Oct): an ear-clipping ladder of reflex-only tests, convex shortcut, strip output, buckets, branchless counts and bitmask hits takes 2,931 ms (CDT) down to 489 ms; earstrip, ISC, is about 14% faster than earcut.hpp natively and 1.4–2.1× faster than earcut.js (graphics, 8).
- 2395 Don't throw it all away: Caching an immediate-mode UI — Moritz Falk (Blick blog, 21 Oct): GPU-binned 64-byte command stream, per-tile ordered hashes, repaint only changed tiles, SDF shape groups, alpha barriers in registers; about 94% fewer tiles painted and about 20% lower CPU+GPU power in a short test (graphics, 9).
- 2396 An SPMC Broadcast Ring in Shared Memory — Jasper (blog, 4 Oct): stamp-in-slot wait-free broadcast ring; torn-read fix needs invalidate-then-write-then-stamp; measured per-reader coherence cost; shm pitfalls (no pointers, pre-fault, cursor persistence) (systems, 8).
- 2397 Demystifying Spherical Harmonics: An Optimization Approach — Andrew Helmer (blog, 15 Oct, updated 19 Oct): SH projection as least squares with rational coefficients; shows that the clamp, squared-error and hemisphere-domain violations bias probes and lightmaps (graphics, 8).
- 2398 Fast blur with animated radius — Aras Pranckevičius (aras-p.info, 1 Oct, in GPW 459): "Smol Gaussian", a variance-budgeted downsample/blur/B-spline reconstruct for animated, anisotropic HDR blur; WebGPU playground plus Blender PR 164496 (graphics, 7).
