- Method: **forward citation / bibliographic-coupling sweep**. The 11-18 and 11-20 runs went *backward* (what the library's cards cite). This run went *forward*: for every pre-2010 card with a non-arXiv DOI (754 seeds, 721 resolved in OpenAlex), it pulled the works that **cite** the seed, published before 2018, sorted by citation count, up to the top 60 per seed. Dedupe: normalized title against cards, summaries, every string in seen.json and every run log, plus first-author surname + year against card frontmatter, then a distinctive-term grep of the library for each shortlisted item.
